سورة التكاثر102. At-Takathur · The Rivalry in world increase
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
أَلۡهَىٰكُمُ ٱلتَّكَاثُرُ١
Alhakumu alttakathuru
102:1Rivalry in worldly increase distracteth you
حَتَّىٰ زُرۡتُمُ ٱلۡمَقَابِرَ٢
Hatta zurtumu almaqabira
102:2Until ye come to the graves
كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ٣
Kalla sawfa taAAlamoona
102:3Nay, but ye will come to know
ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَوۡفَ تَعۡلَمُونَ٤
Thumma kalla sawfa taAAlamoona
102:4Nay, but ye will come to know
كَلَّا لَوۡ تَعۡلَمُونَ عِلۡمَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ٥
Kalla law taAAlamoona AAilma alyaqeeni
102:5Nay, would that ye knew (now) with a sure knowledge
لَتَرَوُنَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ٦
Latarawunna aljaheema
102:6For ye will behold hell-fire
ثُمَّ لَتَرَوُنَّهَا عَيۡنَ ٱلۡيَقِينِ٧
Thumma latarawunnaha AAayna alyaqeeni
102:7Aye, ye will behold it with sure vision
ثُمَّ لَتُسۡـَٔلُنَّ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَنِ ٱلنَّعِيمِ٨
Thumma latusalunna yawmaithin AAani alnnaAAeemi
102:8Then, on that day, ye will be asked concerning pleasure
