سورة الليل92. Al-Layl · The Night
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰ١
Waallayli itha yaghsha
92:1By the night enshrouding
وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا تَجَلَّىٰ٢
Waalnnahari itha tajalla
92:2And the day resplendent
وَمَا خَلَقَ ٱلذَّكَرَ وَٱلۡأُنثَىٰٓ٣
Wama khalaqa alththakara waalontha
92:3And Him Who hath created male and female
إِنَّ سَعۡيَكُمۡ لَشَتَّىٰ٤
Inna saAAyakum lashatta
92:4Lo! your effort is dispersed (toward divers ends)
فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أَعۡطَىٰ وَٱتَّقَىٰ٥
Faamma man aAAta waittaqa
92:5As for him who giveth and is dutiful (toward Allah)
وَصَدَّقَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ٦
Wasaddaqa bialhusna
92:6And believeth in goodness
فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ٧
Fasanuyassiruhu lilyusra
92:7Surely We will ease his way unto the state of ease
وَأَمَّا مَنۢ بَخِلَ وَٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ٨
Waamma man bakhila waistaghna
92:8But as for him who hoardeth and deemeth himself independent
وَكَذَّبَ بِٱلۡحُسۡنَىٰ٩
Wakaththaba bialhusna
92:9And disbelieveth in goodness
فَسَنُيَسِّرُهُۥ لِلۡعُسۡرَىٰ١٠
Fasanuyassiruhu lilAAusra
92:10Surely We will ease his way unto adversity
وَمَا يُغۡنِي عَنۡهُ مَالُهُۥٓ إِذَا تَرَدَّىٰٓ١١
Wama yughnee AAanhu maluhu itha taradda
92:11His riches will not save him when he perisheth
إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا لَلۡهُدَىٰ١٢
Inna AAalayna lalhuda
92:12Lo! Ours it is (to give) the guidance
وَإِنَّ لَنَا لَلۡأٓخِرَةَ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰ١٣
Wainna lana lalakhirata waaloola
92:13And lo! unto Us belong the latter portion and the former
فَأَنذَرۡتُكُمۡ نَارٗا تَلَظَّىٰ١٤
Faanthartukum naran talaththa
92:14Therefor have I warned you of the flaming Fire
لَا يَصۡلَىٰهَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡأَشۡقَى١٥
La yaslaha illa alashqa
92:15Which only the most wretched must endure
ٱلَّذِي كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰ١٦
Allathee kaththaba watawalla
92:16He who denieth and turneth away
وَسَيُجَنَّبُهَا ٱلۡأَتۡقَى١٧
Wasayujannabuha alatqa
92:17Far removed from it will be the righteous
ٱلَّذِي يُؤۡتِي مَالَهُۥ يَتَزَكَّىٰ١٨
Allathee yutee malahu yatazakka
92:18Who giveth his wealth that he may grow (in goodness)
وَمَا لِأَحَدٍ عِندَهُۥ مِن نِّعۡمَةٖ تُجۡزَىٰٓ١٩
Wama liahadin AAindahu min niAAmatin tujza
92:19And none hath with him any favour for reward
إِلَّا ٱبۡتِغَآءَ وَجۡهِ رَبِّهِ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ٢٠
Illa ibtighaa wajhi rabbihi alaAAla
92:20Except as seeking (to fulfil) the purpose of his Lord Most High
وَلَسَوۡفَ يَرۡضَىٰ٢١
Walasawfa yarda
92:21He verily will be content
