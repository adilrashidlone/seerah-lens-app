سورة الكوثر108. Al-Kawthar · The Abundance
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
إِنَّآ أَعۡطَيۡنَٰكَ ٱلۡكَوۡثَرَ١
Inna aAAtaynaka alkawthara
108:1Lo! We have given thee Abundance
فَصَلِّ لِرَبِّكَ وَٱنۡحَرۡ٢
Fasalli lirabbika wainhar
108:2So pray unto thy Lord, and sacrifice
إِنَّ شَانِئَكَ هُوَ ٱلۡأَبۡتَرُ٣
Inna shaniaka huwa alabtaru
108:3Lo! it is thy insulter (and not thou) who is without posterity
