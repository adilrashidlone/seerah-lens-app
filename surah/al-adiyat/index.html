سورة العاديات100. Al-'Adiyat · The Courser
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلۡعَٰدِيَٰتِ ضَبۡحٗا١
WaalAAadiyati dabhan
100:1By the snorting courses
فَٱلۡمُورِيَٰتِ قَدۡحٗا٢
Faalmooriyati qadhan
100:2Striking sparks of fire
فَٱلۡمُغِيرَٰتِ صُبۡحٗا٣
Faalmugheerati subhan
100:3And scouring to the raid at dawn
فَأَثَرۡنَ بِهِۦ نَقۡعٗا٤
Faatharna bihi naqAAan
100:4Then, therewith, with their trail of dust
فَوَسَطۡنَ بِهِۦ جَمۡعًا٥
Fawasatna bihi jamAAan
100:5Cleaving, as one, the centre (of the foe)
إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لِرَبِّهِۦ لَكَنُودٞ٦
Inna alinsana lirabbihi lakanoodun
100:6Lo! man is an ingrate unto his Lord
وَإِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ ذَٰلِكَ لَشَهِيدٞ٧
Wainnahu AAala thalika lashaheedun
100:7And lo! he is a witness unto that
وَإِنَّهُۥ لِحُبِّ ٱلۡخَيۡرِ لَشَدِيدٌ٨
Wainnahu lihubbi alkhayri lashadeedun
100:8And lo! in the love of wealth he is violent
۞أَفَلَا يَعۡلَمُ إِذَا بُعۡثِرَ مَا فِي ٱلۡقُبُورِ٩
Afala yaAAlamu itha buAAthira ma fee alquboori
100:9Knoweth he not that, when the contents of the graves are poured forth
وَحُصِّلَ مَا فِي ٱلصُّدُورِ١٠
Wahussila ma fee alssudoori
100:10And the secrets of the breasts are made known
إِنَّ رَبَّهُم بِهِمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لَّخَبِيرُۢ١١
Inna rabbahum bihim yawmaithin lakhabeerun
100:11On that day will their Lord be perfectly informed concerning them
