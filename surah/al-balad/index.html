سورة البلد90. Al-Balad · The City
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
لَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ١
La oqsimu bihatha albaladi
90:1Nay, I swear by this city
وَأَنتَ حِلُّۢ بِهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ٢
Waanta hillun bihatha albaladi
90:2And thou art an indweller of this city
وَوَالِدٖ وَمَا وَلَدَ٣
Wawalidin wama walada
90:3And the begetter and that which he begat
لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِي كَبَدٍ٤
Laqad khalaqna alinsana fee kabadin
90:4We verily have created man in an atmosphere
أَيَحۡسَبُ أَن لَّن يَقۡدِرَ عَلَيۡهِ أَحَدٞ٥
Ayahsabu an lan yaqdira AAalayhi ahadun
90:5Thinketh he that none hath power over him
يَقُولُ أَهۡلَكۡتُ مَالٗا لُّبَدًا٦
Yaqoolu ahlaktu malan lubadan
90:6And he saith: I have destroyed vast wealth
أَيَحۡسَبُ أَن لَّمۡ يَرَهُۥٓ أَحَدٌ٧
Ayahsabu an lam yarahu ahadun
90:7Thinketh he that none beholdeth him
أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَل لَّهُۥ عَيۡنَيۡنِ٨
Alam najAAal lahu AAaynayni
90:8Did We not assign unto him two eyes
وَلِسَانٗا وَشَفَتَيۡنِ٩
Walisanan washafatayni
90:9And a tongue and two lips
وَهَدَيۡنَٰهُ ٱلنَّجۡدَيۡنِ١٠
Wahadaynahu alnnajdayni
90:10And guide him to the parting of the mountain ways
فَلَا ٱقۡتَحَمَ ٱلۡعَقَبَةَ١١
Fala iqtahama alAAaqabata
90:11But he hath not attempted the Ascent
وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡعَقَبَةُ١٢
Wama adraka ma alAAaqabatu
90:12Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Ascent is
فَكُّ رَقَبَةٍ١٣
Fakku raqabatin
90:13(It is) to free a slave
أَوۡ إِطۡعَٰمٞ فِي يَوۡمٖ ذِي مَسۡغَبَةٖ١٤
Aw itAAamun fee yawmin thee masghabatin
90:14And to feed in the day of hunger
يَتِيمٗا ذَا مَقۡرَبَةٍ١٥
Yateeman tha maqrabatin
90:15An orphan near of kin
أَوۡ مِسۡكِينٗا ذَا مَتۡرَبَةٖ١٦
Aw miskeenan tha matrabatin
90:16Or some poor wretch in misery
ثُمَّ كَانَ مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡمَرۡحَمَةِ١٧
Thumma kana mina allatheena amanoo watawasaw bialssabri watawasaw bialmarhamati
90:17And to be of those who believe and exhort one another to perseverance and exhort one another to pity
أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَيۡمَنَةِ١٨
Olaika ashabu almaymanati
90:18Their place will be on the right hand
وَٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا هُمۡ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡمَشۡـَٔمَةِ١٩
Waallatheena kafaroo biayatina hum ashabu almashamati
90:19But those who disbelieve Our revelations, their place will be on the left hand
عَلَيۡهِمۡ نَارٞ مُّؤۡصَدَةُۢ٢٠
AAalayhim narun musadatun
90:20Fire will be an awning over them
