سورة العصر103. Al-'Asr · The Declining Day
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلۡعَصۡرِ١
WaalAAasri
103:1By the declining day
إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَفِي خُسۡرٍ٢
Inna alinsana lafee khusrin
103:2Lo! man is a state of loss
إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلۡحَقِّ وَتَوَاصَوۡاْ بِٱلصَّبۡرِ٣
Illa allatheena amanoo waAAamiloo alssalihati watawasaw bialhaqqi watawasaw bialssabri
103:3Save those who believe and do good works, and exhort one another to truth and exhort one another to endurance
