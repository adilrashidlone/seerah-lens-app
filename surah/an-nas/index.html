سورة الناس114. An-Nas · Mankind
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلنَّاسِ١
Qul aAAoothu birabbi alnnasi
114:1Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of mankind
مَلِكِ ٱلنَّاسِ٢
Maliki alnnasi
114:2The King of mankind
إِلَٰهِ ٱلنَّاسِ٣
Ilahi alnnasi
114:3The god of mankind
مِن شَرِّ ٱلۡوَسۡوَاسِ ٱلۡخَنَّاسِ٤
Min sharri alwaswasi alkhannasi
114:4From the evil of the sneaking whisperer
ٱلَّذِي يُوَسۡوِسُ فِي صُدُورِ ٱلنَّاسِ٥
Allathee yuwaswisu fee sudoori alnnasi
114:5Who whispereth in the hearts of mankind
مِنَ ٱلۡجِنَّةِ وَٱلنَّاسِ٦
Mina aljinnati wa alnnasm
114:6Of the jinn and of mankind
