سورة الشرح94. Ash-Sharh · The Relief
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
أَلَمۡ نَشۡرَحۡ لَكَ صَدۡرَكَ١
Alam nashrah laka sadraka
94:1Have We not caused thy bosom to dilate
وَوَضَعۡنَا عَنكَ وِزۡرَكَ٢
WawadaAAna AAanka wizraka
94:2And eased thee of the burden
ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنقَضَ ظَهۡرَكَ٣
Allathee anqada thahraka
94:3Which weighed down thy back
وَرَفَعۡنَا لَكَ ذِكۡرَكَ٤
WarafaAAna laka thikraka
94:4And exalted thy fame
فَإِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرًا٥
Fainna maAAa alAAusri yusran
94:5But lo! with hardship goeth ease
إِنَّ مَعَ ٱلۡعُسۡرِ يُسۡرٗا٦
Inna maAAa alAAusri yusran
94:6Lo! with hardship goeth ease
فَإِذَا فَرَغۡتَ فَٱنصَبۡ٧
Faitha faraghta fainsab
94:7So when thou art relieved, still toil
وَإِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَٱرۡغَب٨
Waila rabbika fairghab
94:8And strive to please thy Lord
