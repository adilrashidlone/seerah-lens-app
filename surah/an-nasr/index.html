سورة النصر110. An-Nasr · The Divine Support
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
إِذَا جَآءَ نَصۡرُ ٱللَّهِ وَٱلۡفَتۡحُ١
Itha jaa nasru Allahi waalfathu
110:1When Allah's succour and the triumph cometh
وَرَأَيۡتَ ٱلنَّاسَ يَدۡخُلُونَ فِي دِينِ ٱللَّهِ أَفۡوَاجٗا٢
Waraayta alnnasa yadkhuloona fee deeni Allahi afwajan
110:2And thou seest mankind entering the religion of Allah in troops
فَسَبِّحۡ بِحَمۡدِ رَبِّكَ وَٱسۡتَغۡفِرۡهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ تَوَّابَۢا٣
Fasabbih bihamdi rabbika waistaghfirhu innahu kana tawwaban
110:3Then hymn the praises of thy Lord, and seek forgiveness of Him. Lo! He is ever ready to show mercy
