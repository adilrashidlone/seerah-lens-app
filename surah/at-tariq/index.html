سورة الطارق86. At-Tariq · The Nightcommer
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَٱلطَّارِقِ١
Waalssamai waalttariqi
86:1By the heaven and the Morning Star
وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلطَّارِقُ٢
Wama adraka ma alttariqu
86:2Ah, what will tell thee what the Morning Star is
ٱلنَّجۡمُ ٱلثَّاقِبُ٣
Alnnajmu alththaqibu
86:3The piercing Star
إِن كُلُّ نَفۡسٖ لَّمَّا عَلَيۡهَا حَافِظٞ٤
In kullu nafsin lamma AAalayha hafithun
86:4No human soul but hath a guardian over it
فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مِمَّ خُلِقَ٥
Falyanthuri alinsanu mimma khuliqa
86:5So let man consider from what he is created
خُلِقَ مِن مَّآءٖ دَافِقٖ٦
Khuliqa min main dafiqin
86:6He is created from a gushing fluid
يَخۡرُجُ مِنۢ بَيۡنِ ٱلصُّلۡبِ وَٱلتَّرَآئِبِ٧
Yakhruju min bayni alssulbi waalttaraibi
86:7That issued from between the loins and ribs
إِنَّهُۥ عَلَىٰ رَجۡعِهِۦ لَقَادِرٞ٨
Innahu AAala rajAAihi laqadirun
86:8Lo! He verily is Able to return him (unto life)
يَوۡمَ تُبۡلَى ٱلسَّرَآئِرُ٩
Yawma tubla alssarairu
86:9On the day when hidden thoughts shall be searched out
فَمَا لَهُۥ مِن قُوَّةٖ وَلَا نَاصِرٖ١٠
Fama lahu min quwwatin wala nasirin
86:10Then will he have no might nor any helper
وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلرَّجۡعِ١١
Waalssamai thati alrrajAAi
86:11By the heaven which giveth the returning rain
وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ ذَاتِ ٱلصَّدۡعِ١٢
Waalardi thati alssadAAi
86:12And the earth which splitteth (with the growth of trees and plants)
إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلٞ فَصۡلٞ١٣
Innahu laqawlun faslun
86:13Lo! this (Qur'an) is a conclusive word
وَمَا هُوَ بِٱلۡهَزۡلِ١٤
Wama huwa bialhazli
86:14It is no pleasantry
إِنَّهُمۡ يَكِيدُونَ كَيۡدٗا١٥
Innahum yakeedoona kaydan
86:15Lo! they plot a plot (against thee, O Muhammad)
وَأَكِيدُ كَيۡدٗا١٦
Waakeedu kaydan
86:16And I plot a plot (against them)
فَمَهِّلِ ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ أَمۡهِلۡهُمۡ رُوَيۡدَۢا١٧
Famahhili alkafireena amhilhum ruwaydan
86:17So give a respite to the disbelievers. Deal thou gently with them for a while
