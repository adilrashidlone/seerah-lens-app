سورة الماعون107. Al-Ma'un · The Small kindnesses
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
أَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي يُكَذِّبُ بِٱلدِّينِ١
Araayta allathee yukaththibu bialddeeni
107:1Hast thou observed him who belieth religion
فَذَٰلِكَ ٱلَّذِي يَدُعُّ ٱلۡيَتِيمَ٢
Fathalika allathee yaduAAAAu alyateema
107:2That is he who repelleth the orphan
وَلَا يَحُضُّ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ٣
Wala yahuddu AAala taAAami almiskeeni
107:3And urgeth not the feeding of the needy
فَوَيۡلٞ لِّلۡمُصَلِّينَ٤
Fawaylun lilmusalleena
107:4Ah, woe unto worshippers
ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ عَن صَلَاتِهِمۡ سَاهُونَ٥
Allatheena hum AAan salatihim sahoona
107:5Who are heedless of their prayer
ٱلَّذِينَ هُمۡ يُرَآءُونَ٦
Allatheena hum yuraoona
107:6Who would be seen (at worship)
وَيَمۡنَعُونَ ٱلۡمَاعُونَ٧
WayamnaAAoona almaAAoona
107:7Yet refuse small kindnesses
