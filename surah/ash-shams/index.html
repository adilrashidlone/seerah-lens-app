سورة الشمس91. Ash-Shams · The Sun
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلشَّمۡسِ وَضُحَىٰهَا١
Waalshshamsi waduhaha
91:1By the sun and his brightness
وَٱلۡقَمَرِ إِذَا تَلَىٰهَا٢
Waalqamari itha talaha
91:2And the moon when she followeth him
وَٱلنَّهَارِ إِذَا جَلَّىٰهَا٣
Waalnnahari itha jallaha
91:3And the day when it revealeth him
وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَغۡشَىٰهَا٤
Waallayli itha yaghshaha
91:4And the night when it enshroudeth him
وَٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا بَنَىٰهَا٥
Waalssamai wama banaha
91:5And the heaven and Him Who built it
وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا طَحَىٰهَا٦
Waalardi wama tahaha
91:6And the earth and Him Who spread it
وَنَفۡسٖ وَمَا سَوَّىٰهَا٧
Wanafsin wama sawwaha
91:7And a soul and Him Who perfected it
فَأَلۡهَمَهَا فُجُورَهَا وَتَقۡوَىٰهَا٨
Faalhamaha fujooraha wataqwaha
91:8And inspired it (with conscience of) what is wrong for it and (what is) right for it
قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن زَكَّىٰهَا٩
Qad aflaha man zakkaha
91:9He is indeed successful who causeth it to grow
وَقَدۡ خَابَ مَن دَسَّىٰهَا١٠
Waqad khaba man dassaha
91:10And he is indeed a failure who stunteth it
كَذَّبَتۡ ثَمُودُ بِطَغۡوَىٰهَآ١١
Kaththabat thamoodu bitaghwaha
91:11(The tribe of) Thamud denied (the truth) in their rebellious pride
إِذِ ٱنۢبَعَثَ أَشۡقَىٰهَا١٢
Ithi inbaAAatha ashqaha
91:12When the basest of them broke forth
فَقَالَ لَهُمۡ رَسُولُ ٱللَّهِ نَاقَةَ ٱللَّهِ وَسُقۡيَٰهَا١٣
Faqala lahum rasoolu Allahi naqata Allahi wasuqyaha
91:13And the messenger of Allah said: It is the she-camel of Allah, so let her drink
فَكَذَّبُوهُ فَعَقَرُوهَا فَدَمۡدَمَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّهُم بِذَنۢبِهِمۡ فَسَوَّىٰهَا١٤
Fakaththaboohu faAAaqarooha fadamdama AAalayhim rabbuhum bithanbihim fasawwaha
91:14But they denied him, and they hamstrung her, so Allah doomed them for their sin and rased (their dwellings)
وَلَا يَخَافُ عُقۡبَٰهَا١٥
Wala yakhafu AAuqbaha
91:15He dreadeth not the sequel (of events)
