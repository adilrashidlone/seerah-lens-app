سورة الأعلى87. Al-A'la · The Most High
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
سَبِّحِ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّكَ ٱلۡأَعۡلَى١
Sabbihi isma rabbika alaAAla
87:1Praise the name of thy Lord the Most High
ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ٢
Allathee khalaqa fasawwa
87:2Who createth, then disposeth
وَٱلَّذِي قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ٣
Waallathee qaddara fahada
87:3Who measureth, then guideth
وَٱلَّذِيٓ أَخۡرَجَ ٱلۡمَرۡعَىٰ٤
Waallathee akhraja almarAAa
87:4Who bringeth forth the pasturage
فَجَعَلَهُۥ غُثَآءً أَحۡوَىٰ٥
FajaAAalahu ghuthaan ahwa
87:5Then turneth it to russet stubble
سَنُقۡرِئُكَ فَلَا تَنسَىٰٓ٦
Sanuqrioka fala tansa
87:6We shall make thee read (O Muhammad) so that thou shalt not forget
إِلَّا مَا شَآءَ ٱللَّهُۚ إِنَّهُۥ يَعۡلَمُ ٱلۡجَهۡرَ وَمَا يَخۡفَىٰ٧
Illa ma shaa Allahu innahu yaAAlamu aljahra wama yakhfa
87:7Save that which Allah willeth. Lo! He knoweth the disclosed and that which still is hidden
وَنُيَسِّرُكَ لِلۡيُسۡرَىٰ٨
Wanuyassiruka lilyusra
87:8And We shall ease thy way unto the state of ease
فَذَكِّرۡ إِن نَّفَعَتِ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ٩
Fathakkir in nafaAAati alththikra
87:9Therefor remind (men), for of use is the reminder
سَيَذَّكَّرُ مَن يَخۡشَىٰ١٠
Sayaththakkaru man yakhsha
87:10He will heed who feareth
وَيَتَجَنَّبُهَا ٱلۡأَشۡقَى١١
Wayatajannabuha alashqa
87:11But the most hapless will flout it
ٱلَّذِي يَصۡلَى ٱلنَّارَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ١٢
Allathee yasla alnnara alkubra
87:12He who will be flung to the great Fire
ثُمَّ لَا يَمُوتُ فِيهَا وَلَا يَحۡيَىٰ١٣
Thumma la yamootu feeha wala yahya
87:13Wherein he will neither die nor live
قَدۡ أَفۡلَحَ مَن تَزَكَّىٰ١٤
Qad aflaha man tazakka
87:14He is successful who groweth
وَذَكَرَ ٱسۡمَ رَبِّهِۦ فَصَلَّىٰ١٥
Wathakara isma rabbihi fasalla
87:15And remembereth the name of his Lord, so prayeth
بَلۡ تُؤۡثِرُونَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا١٦
Bal tuthiroona alhayata alddunya
87:16But ye prefer the life of the world
وَٱلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرٞ وَأَبۡقَىٰٓ١٧
Waalakhiratu khayrun waabqa
87:17Although the Hereafter is better and more lasting
إِنَّ هَٰذَا لَفِي ٱلصُّحُفِ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ١٨
Inna hatha lafee alssuhufi aloola
87:18Lo! This is in the former scrolls
صُحُفِ إِبۡرَٰهِيمَ وَمُوسَىٰ١٩
Suhufi ibraheema wamoosa
87:19The Books of Abraham and Moses
