سورة القدر97. Al-Qadr · The Power
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ فِي لَيۡلَةِ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ١
Inna anzalnahu fee laylati alqadri
97:1Lo! We revealed it on the Night of Predestination
وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ٢
Wama adraka ma laylatu alqadri
97:2Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Night of Power is
لَيۡلَةُ ٱلۡقَدۡرِ خَيۡرٞ مِّنۡ أَلۡفِ شَهۡرٖ٣
Laylatu alqadri khayrun min alfi shahrin
97:3The Night of Power is better than a thousand months
تَنَزَّلُ ٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ وَٱلرُّوحُ فِيهَا بِإِذۡنِ رَبِّهِم مِّن كُلِّ أَمۡرٖ٤
Tanazzalu almalaikatu waalrroohu feeha biithni rabbihim min kulli amrin
97:4The angels and the Spirit descend therein, by the permission of their Lord, with all decrees
سَلَٰمٌ هِيَ حَتَّىٰ مَطۡلَعِ ٱلۡفَجۡرِ٥
Salamun hiya hatta matlaAAi alfajri
97:5(The night is) Peace until the rising of the dawn
