سورة الزلزلة99. Az-Zalzalah · The Earthquake
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
إِذَا زُلۡزِلَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ زِلۡزَالَهَا١
Itha zulzilati alardu zilzalaha
99:1When Earth is shaken with her (final) earthquake
وَأَخۡرَجَتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ أَثۡقَالَهَا٢
Waakhrajati alardu athqalaha
99:2And Earth yieldeth up her burdens
وَقَالَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا لَهَا٣
Waqala alinsanu ma laha
99:3And man saith: What aileth her
يَوۡمَئِذٖ تُحَدِّثُ أَخۡبَارَهَا٤
Yawmaithin tuhaddithu akhbaraha
99:4That day she will relate her chronicles
بِأَنَّ رَبَّكَ أَوۡحَىٰ لَهَا٥
Bianna rabbaka awha laha
99:5Because thy Lord inspireth her
يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَصۡدُرُ ٱلنَّاسُ أَشۡتَاتٗا لِّيُرَوۡاْ أَعۡمَٰلَهُمۡ٦
Yawmaithin yasduru alnnasu ashtatan liyuraw aAAmalahum
99:6That day mankind will issue forth in scattered groups to be shown their deeds
فَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٍ خَيۡرٗا يَرَهُۥ٧
Faman yaAAmal mithqala tharratin khayran yarahu
99:7And whoso doeth good an atom's weight will see it then
وَمَن يَعۡمَلۡ مِثۡقَالَ ذَرَّةٖ شَرّٗا يَرَهُۥ٨
Waman yaAAmal mithqala tharratin sharran yarahu
99:8And whoso doeth ill an atom's weight will see it then
