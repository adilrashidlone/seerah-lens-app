سورة الهمزة104. Al-Humazah · The Traducer
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَيۡلٞ لِّكُلِّ هُمَزَةٖ لُّمَزَةٍ١
Waylun likulli humazatin lumazatin
104:1Woe unto every slandering traducer
ٱلَّذِي جَمَعَ مَالٗا وَعَدَّدَهُۥ٢
Allathee jamaAAa malan waAAaddadahu
104:2Who hath gathered wealth (of this world) and arranged it
يَحۡسَبُ أَنَّ مَالَهُۥٓ أَخۡلَدَهُۥ٣
Yahsabu anna malahu akhladahu
104:3He thinketh that his wealth will render him immortal
كَلَّاۖ لَيُنۢبَذَنَّ فِي ٱلۡحُطَمَةِ٤
Kalla layunbathanna fee alhutamati
104:4Nay, but verily he will be flung to the Consuming One
وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡحُطَمَةُ٥
Wama adraka ma alhutamatu
104:5Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Consuming One is
نَارُ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡمُوقَدَةُ٦
Naru Allahi almooqadatu
104:6(It is) the fire of Allah, kindled
ٱلَّتِي تَطَّلِعُ عَلَى ٱلۡأَفۡـِٔدَةِ٧
Allatee tattaliAAu AAala alafidati
104:7Which leapeth up over the hearts (of men)
إِنَّهَا عَلَيۡهِم مُّؤۡصَدَةٞ٨
lnnaha AAalayhim musadatun
104:8Lo! it is closed in on them
فِي عَمَدٖ مُّمَدَّدَةِۭ٩
Fee AAamadin mumaddadatin
104:9In outstretched columns
