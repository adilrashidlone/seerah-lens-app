Surah An-Nazi'at: Arabic, Transliteration and English Meaning

Surah An-Nazi'at (النازعات, “Those who drag forth”) is surah 79 of the Quran, a Makki surah of 46 ayat. Read it below in Arabic with transliteration and the English meaning.

In Seerah Lens, Surah An-Nazi'at comes with a sourced moment from the life of the Prophet ﷺ: Sent with the Hour, like these two fingers.

سورة النازعات79. An-Nazi'at · Those who drag forth
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلنَّـٰزِعَٰتِ غَرۡقٗا١
WaalnnaziAAati gharqan
79:1By those who drag forth to destruction
وَٱلنَّـٰشِطَٰتِ نَشۡطٗا٢
Waalnnashitati nashtan
79:2By the meteors rushing
وَٱلسَّـٰبِحَٰتِ سَبۡحٗا٣
Waalssabihati sabhan
79:3By the lone stars floating
فَٱلسَّـٰبِقَٰتِ سَبۡقٗا٤
Faalssabiqati sabqan
79:4By the angels hastening
فَٱلۡمُدَبِّرَٰتِ أَمۡرٗا٥
Faalmudabbirati amran
79:5And those who govern the event
يَوۡمَ تَرۡجُفُ ٱلرَّاجِفَةُ٦
Yawma tarjufu alrrajifatu
79:6On the day when the first trump resoundeth
تَتۡبَعُهَا ٱلرَّادِفَةُ٧
TatbaAAuha alrradifatu
79:7And the second followeth it
قُلُوبٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ وَاجِفَةٌ٨
Quloobun yawmaithin wajifatun
79:8On that day hearts beat painfully
أَبۡصَٰرُهَا خَٰشِعَةٞ٩
Absaruha khashiAAatun
79:9While eyes are downcast
يَقُولُونَ أَءِنَّا لَمَرۡدُودُونَ فِي ٱلۡحَافِرَةِ١٠
Yaqooloona ainna lamardoodoona fee alhafirati
79:10(Now) they are saying: Shall we really be restored to our first state
أَءِذَا كُنَّا عِظَٰمٗا نَّخِرَةٗ١١
Aitha kunna AAithaman nakhiratan
79:11Even after we are crumbled bones
قَالُواْ تِلۡكَ إِذٗا كَرَّةٌ خَاسِرَةٞ١٢
Qaloo tilka ithan karratun khasiratun
79:12They say: Then that would be a vain proceeding
فَإِنَّمَا هِيَ زَجۡرَةٞ وَٰحِدَةٞ١٣
Fainnama hiya zajratun wahidatun
79:13Surely it will need but one shout
فَإِذَا هُم بِٱلسَّاهِرَةِ١٤
Faitha hum bialssahirati
79:14And lo! they will be awakened
هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ مُوسَىٰٓ١٥
Hal ataka hadeethu moosa
79:15Hath there come unto thee the history of Moses
إِذۡ نَادَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ بِٱلۡوَادِ ٱلۡمُقَدَّسِ طُوًى١٦
Ith nadahu rabbuhu bialwadi almuqaddasi tuwan
79:16How his Lord called him in the holy vale of Tuwa
ٱذۡهَبۡ إِلَىٰ فِرۡعَوۡنَ إِنَّهُۥ طَغَىٰ١٧
Ithhab ila firAAawna innahu tagha
79:17(Saying:) Go thou unto Pharaoh - Lo! he hath rebelled
فَقُلۡ هَل لَّكَ إِلَىٰٓ أَن تَزَكَّىٰ١٨
Faqul hal laka ila an tazakka
79:18And say (unto him): Hast thou (will) to grow (in grace)
وَأَهۡدِيَكَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ فَتَخۡشَىٰ١٩
Waahdiyaka ila rabbika fatakhsha
79:19Then I will guide thee to thy Lord and thou shalt fear (Him)
فَأَرَىٰهُ ٱلۡأٓيَةَ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ٢٠
Faarahu alayata alkubra
79:20And he showed him the tremendous token
فَكَذَّبَ وَعَصَىٰ٢١
Fakaththaba waAAasa
79:21But he denied and disobeyed
ثُمَّ أَدۡبَرَ يَسۡعَىٰ٢٢
Thumma adbara yasAAa
79:22Then turned he away in haste
فَحَشَرَ فَنَادَىٰ٢٣
Fahashara fanada
79:23Then gathered he and summoned
فَقَالَ أَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمُ ٱلۡأَعۡلَىٰ٢٤
Faqala ana rabbukumu alaAAla
79:24And proclaimed: "I (Pharaoh) am your Lord the Highest
فَأَخَذَهُ ٱللَّهُ نَكَالَ ٱلۡأٓخِرَةِ وَٱلۡأُولَىٰٓ٢٥
Faakhathahu Allahu nakala alakhirati waaloola
79:25So Allah seized him (and made him) an example for the after (life) and for the former
إِنَّ فِي ذَٰلِكَ لَعِبۡرَةٗ لِّمَن يَخۡشَىٰٓ٢٦
Inna fee thalika laAAibratan liman yakhsha
79:26Lo! herein is indeed a lesson for him who feareth
ءَأَنتُمۡ أَشَدُّ خَلۡقًا أَمِ ٱلسَّمَآءُۚ بَنَىٰهَا٢٧
Aantum ashaddu khalqan ami alssamao banaha
79:27Are ye the harder to create, or is the heaven that He built
رَفَعَ سَمۡكَهَا فَسَوَّىٰهَا٢٨
RafaAAa samkaha fasawwaha
79:28He raised the height thereof and ordered it
وَأَغۡطَشَ لَيۡلَهَا وَأَخۡرَجَ ضُحَىٰهَا٢٩
Waaghtasha laylaha waakhraja duhaha
79:29And He made dark the night thereof, and He brought forth the morn thereof
وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بَعۡدَ ذَٰلِكَ دَحَىٰهَآ٣٠
Waalarda baAAda thalika dahaha
79:30And after that He spread the earth
أَخۡرَجَ مِنۡهَا مَآءَهَا وَمَرۡعَىٰهَا٣١
Akhraja minha maaha wamarAAaha
79:31And produced therefrom the water thereof and the pasture thereof
وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَرۡسَىٰهَا٣٢
Waaljibala arsaha
79:32And He made fast the hills
مَتَٰعٗا لَّكُمۡ وَلِأَنۡعَٰمِكُمۡ٣٣
MataAAan lakum walianAAamikum
79:33A provision for you and for your cattle
فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلطَّآمَّةُ ٱلۡكُبۡرَىٰ٣٤
Faitha jaati alttammatu alkubra
79:34But when the great disaster cometh
يَوۡمَ يَتَذَكَّرُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا سَعَىٰ٣٥
Yawma yatathakkaru alinsanu ma saAAa
79:35The day when man will call to mind his (whole) endeavour
وَبُرِّزَتِ ٱلۡجَحِيمُ لِمَن يَرَىٰ٣٦
Waburrizati aljaheemu liman yara
79:36And hell will stand forth visible to him who seeth
فَأَمَّا مَن طَغَىٰ٣٧
Faamma man tagha
79:37Then, as for him who rebelled
وَءَاثَرَ ٱلۡحَيَوٰةَ ٱلدُّنۡيَا٣٨
Waathara alhayata alddunya
79:38And chose the life of the world
فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَحِيمَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ٣٩
Fainna aljaheema hiya almawa
79:39Lo! hell will be his home
وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَافَ مَقَامَ رَبِّهِۦ وَنَهَى ٱلنَّفۡسَ عَنِ ٱلۡهَوَىٰ٤٠
Waama man khafa maqama rabbihi wanaha alnnafsa AAani alhawa
79:40But as for him who feared to stand before his Lord and restrained his soul from lust
فَإِنَّ ٱلۡجَنَّةَ هِيَ ٱلۡمَأۡوَىٰ٤١
Fainna aljannata hiya almawa
79:41Lo! the Garden will be his home
يَسۡـَٔلُونَكَ عَنِ ٱلسَّاعَةِ أَيَّانَ مُرۡسَىٰهَا٤٢
Yasaloonaka AAani alssaAAati ayyana mursaha
79:42They ask thee of the Hour: when will it come to port
فِيمَ أَنتَ مِن ذِكۡرَىٰهَآ٤٣
Feema anta min thikraha
79:43Why (ask they)? What hast thou to tell thereof
إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ مُنتَهَىٰهَآ٤٤
Ila rabbika muntahaha
79:44Unto thy Lord belongeth (knowledge of) the term thereof
إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُنذِرُ مَن يَخۡشَىٰهَا٤٥
Innama anta munthiru man yakhshaha
79:45Thou art but a warner unto him who feareth it
كَأَنَّهُمۡ يَوۡمَ يَرَوۡنَهَا لَمۡ يَلۡبَثُوٓاْ إِلَّا عَشِيَّةً أَوۡ ضُحَىٰهَا٤٦
Kaannahum yawma yarawnaha lam yalbathoo illa AAashiyyatan aw duhaha
79:46On the day when they behold it, it will be as if they had but tarried for an evening or the morn thereof
