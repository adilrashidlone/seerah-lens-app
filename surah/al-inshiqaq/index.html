سورة الإنشقاق84. Al-Inshiqaq · The Sundering
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنشَقَّتۡ١
Itha alssamao inshaqqat
84:1When the heaven is split asunder
وَأَذِنَتۡ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتۡ٢
Waathinat lirabbiha wahuqqat
84:2And attentive to her Lord in fear
وَإِذَا ٱلۡأَرۡضُ مُدَّتۡ٣
Waitha alardu muddat
84:3And when the earth is spread out
وَأَلۡقَتۡ مَا فِيهَا وَتَخَلَّتۡ٤
Waalqat ma feeha watakhallat
84:4And hath cast out all that was in her, and is empty
وَأَذِنَتۡ لِرَبِّهَا وَحُقَّتۡ٥
Waathinat lirabbiha wahuqqat
84:5And attentive to her Lord in fear
يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِنَّكَ كَادِحٌ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ كَدۡحٗا فَمُلَٰقِيهِ٦
Ya ayyuha alinsanu innaka kadihun ila rabbika kadhan famulaqeehi
84:6Thou, verily, O man, art working toward thy Lord a work which thou wilt meet (in His presence)
فَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ بِيَمِينِهِۦ٧
Faama man ootiya kitabahu biyameenihi
84:7Then whoso is given his account in his right hand
فَسَوۡفَ يُحَاسَبُ حِسَابٗا يَسِيرٗا٨
Fasawfa yuhasabu hisaban yaseeran
84:8He truly will receive an easy reckoning
وَيَنقَلِبُ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورٗا٩
Wayanqalibu ila ahlihi masrooran
84:9And will return unto his folk in joy
وَأَمَّا مَنۡ أُوتِيَ كِتَٰبَهُۥ وَرَآءَ ظَهۡرِهِۦ١٠
Waamma man ootiya kitabahu waraa thahrihi
84:10But whoso is given his account behind his back
فَسَوۡفَ يَدۡعُواْ ثُبُورٗا١١
Fasawfa yadAAoo thubooran
84:11He surely will invoke destruction
وَيَصۡلَىٰ سَعِيرًا١٢
Wayasla saAAeeran
84:12And be thrown to scorching fire
إِنَّهُۥ كَانَ فِيٓ أَهۡلِهِۦ مَسۡرُورًا١٣
Innahu kana fee ahlihi masrooran
84:13He verily lived joyous with his folk
إِنَّهُۥ ظَنَّ أَن لَّن يَحُورَ١٤
Innahu thanna an lan yahoora
84:14He verily deemed that he would never return (unto Allah)
بَلَىٰٓۚ إِنَّ رَبَّهُۥ كَانَ بِهِۦ بَصِيرٗا١٥
Bala inna rabbahu kana bihi baseeran
84:15Nay, but lo! his Lord is ever looking on him
فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلشَّفَقِ١٦
Fala oqsimu bialshshafaqi
84:16Oh, I swear by the afterglow of sunset
وَٱلَّيۡلِ وَمَا وَسَقَ١٧
Waallayli wama wasaqa
84:17And by the night and all that it enshroudeth
وَٱلۡقَمَرِ إِذَا ٱتَّسَقَ١٨
Waalqamari itha ittasaqa
84:18And by the moon when she is at the full
لَتَرۡكَبُنَّ طَبَقًا عَن طَبَقٖ١٩
Latarkabunna tabaqan AAan tabaqin
84:19That ye shall journey on from plane to plane
فَمَا لَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ٢٠
Fama lahum la yuminoona
84:20What aileth them, then, that they believe not
وَإِذَا قُرِئَ عَلَيۡهِمُ ٱلۡقُرۡءَانُ لَا يَسۡجُدُونَۤ۩٢١
Waitha quria AAalayhimu alquranu la yasjudoona
84:21And, when the Qur'an is recited unto them, worship not (Allah)
بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ يُكَذِّبُونَ٢٢
Bali allatheena kafaroo yukaththiboona
84:22Nay, but those who disbelieve will deny
وَٱللَّهُ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَا يُوعُونَ٢٣
WaAllahu aAAlamu bima yooAAoona
84:23And Allah knoweth best what they are hiding
فَبَشِّرۡهُم بِعَذَابٍ أَلِيمٍ٢٤
Fabashshirhum biAAathabin aleemin
84:24So give them tidings of a painful doom
إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونِۭ٢٥
Illa allatheena amanoo waAAamiloo alssalihati lahum ajrun ghayru mamnoonin
84:25Save those who believe and do good works, for theirs is a reward unfailing
