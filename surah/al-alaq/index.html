سورة العلق96. Al-'Alaq · The Clot
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
ٱقۡرَأۡ بِٱسۡمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ١
Iqra biismi rabbika allathee khalaqa
96:1Read: In the name of thy Lord Who createth
خَلَقَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مِنۡ عَلَقٍ٢
Khalaqa alinsana min AAalaqin
96:2Createth man from a clot
ٱقۡرَأۡ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلۡأَكۡرَمُ٣
Iqra warabbuka alakramu
96:3Read: And thy Lord is the Most Bounteous
ٱلَّذِي عَلَّمَ بِٱلۡقَلَمِ٤
Allathee AAallama bialqalami
96:4Who teacheth by the pen
عَلَّمَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ مَا لَمۡ يَعۡلَمۡ٥
AAallama alinsana ma lam yaAAlam
96:5Teacheth man that which he knew not
كَلَّآ إِنَّ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ لَيَطۡغَىٰٓ٦
Kalla inna alinsana layatgha
96:6Nay, but verily man is rebellious
أَن رَّءَاهُ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰٓ٧
An raahu istaghna
96:7That he thinketh himself independent
إِنَّ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكَ ٱلرُّجۡعَىٰٓ٨
Inna ila rabbika alrrujAAa
96:8Lo! unto thy Lord is the return
أَرَءَيۡتَ ٱلَّذِي يَنۡهَىٰ٩
Araayta allathee yanha
96:9Hast thou seen him who dissuadeth
عَبۡدًا إِذَا صَلَّىٰٓ١٠
AAabdan itha salla
96:10A slave when he prayeth
أَرَءَيۡتَ إِن كَانَ عَلَى ٱلۡهُدَىٰٓ١١
Araayta in kana AAala alhuda
96:11Hast thou seen if he relieth on the guidance (of Allah)
أَوۡ أَمَرَ بِٱلتَّقۡوَىٰٓ١٢
Aw amara bialttaqwa
96:12Or enjoineth piety
أَرَءَيۡتَ إِن كَذَّبَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ١٣
Araayta in kaththaba watawalla
96:13Hast thou seen if he denieth (Allah's guidance) and is froward
أَلَمۡ يَعۡلَم بِأَنَّ ٱللَّهَ يَرَىٰ١٤
Alam yaAAlam bianna Allaha yara
96:14Is he then unaware that Allah seeth
كَلَّا لَئِن لَّمۡ يَنتَهِ لَنَسۡفَعَۢا بِٱلنَّاصِيَةِ١٥
Kalla lain lam yantahi lanasfaAAan bialnnasiyati
96:15Nay, but if he cease not We will seize him by the forelock
نَاصِيَةٖ كَٰذِبَةٍ خَاطِئَةٖ١٦
Nasiyatin kathibatin khatiatin
96:16The lying, sinful forelock
فَلۡيَدۡعُ نَادِيَهُۥ١٧
FalyadAAu nadiyahu
96:17Then let him call upon his henchmen
سَنَدۡعُ ٱلزَّبَانِيَةَ١٨
SanadAAu alzzabaniyata
96:18We will call the guards of hell
كَلَّا لَا تُطِعۡهُ وَٱسۡجُدۡۤ وَٱقۡتَرِب۩١٩
Kalla la tutiAAhu waosjud waiqtarib
96:19Nay, Obey not thou him. But prostrate thyself, and draw near (unto Allah)
