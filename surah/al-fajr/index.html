سورة الفجر89. Al-Fajr · The Dawn
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلۡفَجۡرِ١
Waalfajri
89:1By the Dawn
وَلَيَالٍ عَشۡرٖ٢
Walayalin AAashrin
89:2And ten nights
وَٱلشَّفۡعِ وَٱلۡوَتۡرِ٣
WaalshshafAAi waalwatri
89:3And the Even and the Odd
وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا يَسۡرِ٤
Waallayli itha yasri
89:4And the night when it departeth
هَلۡ فِي ذَٰلِكَ قَسَمٞ لِّذِي حِجۡرٍ٥
Hal fee thalika qasamun lithee hijrin
89:5There surely is an oath for thinking man
أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِعَادٍ٦
Alam tara kayfa faAAala rabbuka biAAadin
89:6Dost thou not consider how thy Lord dealt with (the tribe of) A'ad
إِرَمَ ذَاتِ ٱلۡعِمَادِ٧
Irama thati alAAimadi
89:7With many-columned Iram
ٱلَّتِي لَمۡ يُخۡلَقۡ مِثۡلُهَا فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ٨
Allatee lam yukhlaq mithluha fee albiladi
89:8The like of which was not created in the lands
وَثَمُودَ ٱلَّذِينَ جَابُواْ ٱلصَّخۡرَ بِٱلۡوَادِ٩
Wathamooda allatheena jaboo alssakhra bialwadi
89:9And with (the tribe of) Thamud, who clove the rocks in the valley
وَفِرۡعَوۡنَ ذِي ٱلۡأَوۡتَادِ١٠
WafirAAawna thee alawtadi
89:10And with Pharaoh, firm of might
ٱلَّذِينَ طَغَوۡاْ فِي ٱلۡبِلَٰدِ١١
Allatheena taghaw fee albiladi
89:11Who (all) were rebellious (to Allah) in these lands
فَأَكۡثَرُواْ فِيهَا ٱلۡفَسَادَ١٢
Faaktharoo feeha alfasada
89:12And multiplied iniquity therein
فَصَبَّ عَلَيۡهِمۡ رَبُّكَ سَوۡطَ عَذَابٍ١٣
Fasabba AAalayhim rabbuka sawta AAathabin
89:13Therefore thy Lord poured on them the disaster of His punishment
إِنَّ رَبَّكَ لَبِٱلۡمِرۡصَادِ١٤
Inna rabbaka labialmirsadi
89:14Lo! thy Lord is ever watchful
فَأَمَّا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ رَبُّهُۥ فَأَكۡرَمَهُۥ وَنَعَّمَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَكۡرَمَنِ١٥
Faamma alinsanu itha ma ibtalahu rabbuhu faakramahu wanaAAAAamahu fayaqoolu rabbee akramani
89:15As for man, whenever his Lord trieth him by honouring him, and is gracious unto him, he saith: My Lord honoureth me
وَأَمَّآ إِذَا مَا ٱبۡتَلَىٰهُ فَقَدَرَ عَلَيۡهِ رِزۡقَهُۥ فَيَقُولُ رَبِّيٓ أَهَٰنَنِ١٦
Waamma itha ma ibtalahu faqadara AAalayhi rizqahu fayaqoolu rabbee ahanani
89:16But whenever He trieth him by straitening his means of life, he saith: My Lord despiseth me
كَلَّاۖ بَل لَّا تُكۡرِمُونَ ٱلۡيَتِيمَ١٧
Kalla bal la tukrimoona alyateema
89:17Nay, but ye (for your part) honour not the orphan
وَلَا تَحَـٰٓضُّونَ عَلَىٰ طَعَامِ ٱلۡمِسۡكِينِ١٨
Wala tahaddoona AAala taAAami almiskeeni
89:18And urge not on the feeding of the poor
وَتَأۡكُلُونَ ٱلتُّرَاثَ أَكۡلٗا لَّمّٗا١٩
Watakuloona altturatha aklan lamman
89:19And ye devour heritages with devouring greed
وَتُحِبُّونَ ٱلۡمَالَ حُبّٗا جَمّٗا٢٠
Watuhibboona almala hubban jamman
89:20And love wealth with abounding love
كَلَّآۖ إِذَا دُكَّتِ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ دَكّٗا دَكّٗا٢١
Kalla itha dukkati alardu dakkan dakkan
89:21Nay, but when the earth is ground to atoms, grinding, grinding
وَجَآءَ رَبُّكَ وَٱلۡمَلَكُ صَفّٗا صَفّٗا٢٢
Wajaa rabbuka waalmalaku saffan saffan
89:22And thy Lord shall come with angels, rank on rank
وَجِاْيٓءَ يَوۡمَئِذِۭ بِجَهَنَّمَۚ يَوۡمَئِذٖ يَتَذَكَّرُ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ وَأَنَّىٰ لَهُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰ٢٣
Wajeea yawmaithin bijahannama yawmaithin yatathakkaru alinsanu waanna lahu alththikra
89:23And hell is brought near that day; on that day man will remember, but how will the remembrance (then avail him)
يَقُولُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي قَدَّمۡتُ لِحَيَاتِي٢٤
Yaqoolu ya laytanee qaddamtu lihayatee
89:24He will say: Ah, would that I had sent before me (some provision) for my life
فَيَوۡمَئِذٖ لَّا يُعَذِّبُ عَذَابَهُۥٓ أَحَدٞ٢٥
Fayawmaithin la yuAAaththibu AAathabahu ahadun
89:25None punisheth as He will punish on that day
وَلَا يُوثِقُ وَثَاقَهُۥٓ أَحَدٞ٢٦
Wala yoothiqu wathaqahu ahadun
89:26None bindeth as He then will bind
يَـٰٓأَيَّتُهَا ٱلنَّفۡسُ ٱلۡمُطۡمَئِنَّةُ٢٧
Ya ayyatuha alnnafsu almutmainnatu
89:27But ah! thou soul at peace
ٱرۡجِعِيٓ إِلَىٰ رَبِّكِ رَاضِيَةٗ مَّرۡضِيَّةٗ٢٨
IrjiAAee ila rabbiki radiyatan mardiyyatan
89:28Return unto thy Lord, content in His good pleasure
فَٱدۡخُلِي فِي عِبَٰدِي٢٩
Faodkhulee fee AAibadee
89:29Enter thou among My bondmen
وَٱدۡخُلِي جَنَّتِي٣٠
Waodkhulee jannatee
89:30Enter thou My Garden
