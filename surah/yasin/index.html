سورة يس36. Ya-Sin · Ya Sin
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
يسٓ١
Yaseen
36:1Ya Sin
وَٱلۡقُرۡءَانِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ٢
Waalqurani alhakeemi
36:2By the wise Qur'an
إِنَّكَ لَمِنَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ٣
Innaka lamina almursaleena
36:3Lo! thou art of those sent
عَلَىٰ صِرَٰطٖ مُّسۡتَقِيمٖ٤
AAala siratin mustaqeemin
36:4On a straight path
تَنزِيلَ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ٥
Tanzeela alAAazeezi alrraheemi
36:5A revelation of the Mighty, the Merciful
لِتُنذِرَ قَوۡمٗا مَّآ أُنذِرَ ءَابَآؤُهُمۡ فَهُمۡ غَٰفِلُونَ٦
Litunthira qawman ma onthira abaohum fahum ghafiloona
36:6That thou mayst warn a folk whose fathers were not warned, so they are heedless
لَقَدۡ حَقَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَىٰٓ أَكۡثَرِهِمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ٧
Laqad haqqa alqawlu AAala aktharihim fahum la yuminoona
36:7Already hath the judgment, (for their infidelity) proved true of most of them, for they believe not
إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَا فِيٓ أَعۡنَٰقِهِمۡ أَغۡلَٰلٗا فَهِيَ إِلَى ٱلۡأَذۡقَانِ فَهُم مُّقۡمَحُونَ٨
Inna jaAAalna fee aAAnaqihim aghlalan fahiya ila alathqani fahum muqmahoona
36:8Lo! We have put on their necks carcans reaching unto the chins, so that they are made stiff-necked
وَجَعَلۡنَا مِنۢ بَيۡنِ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ سَدّٗا وَمِنۡ خَلۡفِهِمۡ سَدّٗا فَأَغۡشَيۡنَٰهُمۡ فَهُمۡ لَا يُبۡصِرُونَ٩
WajaAAalna min bayni aydeehim saddan wamin khalfihim saddan faaghshaynahum fahum la yubsiroona
36:9And We have set a bar before them and a bar behind them, and (thus) have covered them so that they see not
وَسَوَآءٌ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَأَنذَرۡتَهُمۡ أَمۡ لَمۡ تُنذِرۡهُمۡ لَا يُؤۡمِنُونَ١٠
Wasawaon AAalayhim aanthartahum am lam tunthirhum la yuminoona
36:10Whether thou warn them or thou warn them not, it is alike for them, for they believe not
إِنَّمَا تُنذِرُ مَنِ ٱتَّبَعَ ٱلذِّكۡرَ وَخَشِيَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنَ بِٱلۡغَيۡبِۖ فَبَشِّرۡهُ بِمَغۡفِرَةٖ وَأَجۡرٖ كَرِيمٍ١١
Innama tunthiru mani ittabaAAa alththikra wakhashiya alrrahmana bialghaybi fabashshirhu bimaghfiratin waajrin kareemin
36:11Thou warnest only him who followeth the Reminder and feareth the Beneficent in secret. To him bear tidings of forgiveness and a rich reward
إِنَّا نَحۡنُ نُحۡيِ ٱلۡمَوۡتَىٰ وَنَكۡتُبُ مَا قَدَّمُواْ وَءَاثَٰرَهُمۡۚ وَكُلَّ شَيۡءٍ أَحۡصَيۡنَٰهُ فِيٓ إِمَامٖ مُّبِينٖ١٢
Inna nahnu nuhyee almawta wanaktubu ma qaddamoo waatharahum wakulla shayin ahsaynahu fee imamin mubeenin
36:12Lo! We it is Who bring the dead to life. We record that which they send before (them, and their footprints. And all things We have kept in a clear Register)
وَٱضۡرِبۡ لَهُم مَّثَلًا أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡقَرۡيَةِ إِذۡ جَآءَهَا ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ١٣
Waidrib lahum mathalan ashaba alqaryati ith jaaha almursaloona
36:13Coin for them a similitude: The people of the city when those sent (from Allah) came unto them
إِذۡ أَرۡسَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡهِمُ ٱثۡنَيۡنِ فَكَذَّبُوهُمَا فَعَزَّزۡنَا بِثَالِثٖ فَقَالُوٓاْ إِنَّآ إِلَيۡكُم مُّرۡسَلُونَ١٤
Ith arsalna ilayhimu ithnayni fakaththaboohuma faAAazzazna bithalithin faqaloo inna ilaykum mursaloona
36:14When We sent unto them twain, and they denied them both, so We reinforced them with a third, and they said: Lo! we have been sent unto you
قَالُواْ مَآ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا بَشَرٞ مِّثۡلُنَا وَمَآ أَنزَلَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ مِن شَيۡءٍ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا تَكۡذِبُونَ١٥
Qaloo ma antum illa basharun mithluna wama anzala alrrahmanu min shayin in antum illa takthiboona
36:15They said: Ye are but mortals like unto us. The Beneficent hath naught revealed. Ye do but lie
قَالُواْ رَبُّنَا يَعۡلَمُ إِنَّآ إِلَيۡكُمۡ لَمُرۡسَلُونَ١٦
Qaloo rabbuna yaAAlamu inna ilaykum lamursaloona
36:16They answered: Our Lord knoweth that we are indeed sent unto you
وَمَا عَلَيۡنَآ إِلَّا ٱلۡبَلَٰغُ ٱلۡمُبِينُ١٧
Wama AAalayna illa albalaghu almubeenu
36:17And our duty is but plain conveyance (of the message)
قَالُوٓاْ إِنَّا تَطَيَّرۡنَا بِكُمۡۖ لَئِن لَّمۡ تَنتَهُواْ لَنَرۡجُمَنَّكُمۡ وَلَيَمَسَّنَّكُم مِّنَّا عَذَابٌ أَلِيمٞ١٨
Qaloo inna tatayyarna bikum lain lam tantahoo lanarjumannakum walayamassannakum minna AAathabun aleemun
36:18(The people of the city) said: We augur ill of you. If ye desist not, we shall surely stone you, and grievous torture will befall you at our hands
قَالُواْ طَـٰٓئِرُكُم مَّعَكُمۡ أَئِن ذُكِّرۡتُمۚ بَلۡ أَنتُمۡ قَوۡمٞ مُّسۡرِفُونَ١٩
Qaloo tairukum maAAakum ain thukkirtum bal antum qawmun musrifoona
36:19They said: Your evil augury be with you! Is it because ye are reminded (of the truth)? Nay, but ye are froward folk
وَجَآءَ مِنۡ أَقۡصَا ٱلۡمَدِينَةِ رَجُلٞ يَسۡعَىٰ قَالَ يَٰقَوۡمِ ٱتَّبِعُواْ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلِينَ٢٠
Wajaa min aqsa almadeenati rajulun yasAAa qala ya qawmi ittabiAAoo almursaleena
36:20And there came from the uttermost part of the city a man running. He cried: O my people! Follow those who have been sent
ٱتَّبِعُواْ مَن لَّا يَسۡـَٔلُكُمۡ أَجۡرٗا وَهُم مُّهۡتَدُونَ٢١
IttabiAAoo man la yasalukum ajran wahum muhtadoona
36:21Follow those who ask of you no fee, and who are rightly guided
وَمَالِيَ لَآ أَعۡبُدُ ٱلَّذِي فَطَرَنِي وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ٢٢
Wama liya la aAAbudu allathee fataranee wailayhi turjaAAoona
36:22For what cause should I not serve Him Who hath created me, and unto Whom ye will be brought back
ءَأَتَّخِذُ مِن دُونِهِۦٓ ءَالِهَةً إِن يُرِدۡنِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ بِضُرّٖ لَّا تُغۡنِ عَنِّي شَفَٰعَتُهُمۡ شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَا يُنقِذُونِ٢٣
Aattakhithu min doonihi alihatan in yuridni alrrahmanu bidurrin la tughni AAannee shafaAAatuhum shayan wala yunqithooni
36:23Shall I take (other) gods in place of Him when, if the Beneficent should wish me any harm, their intercession will avail me naught, nor can they save
إِنِّيٓ إِذٗا لَّفِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٍ٢٤
Innee ithan lafee dalalin mubeenin
36:24Then truly I should be in error manifest
إِنِّيٓ ءَامَنتُ بِرَبِّكُمۡ فَٱسۡمَعُونِ٢٥
Innee amantu birabbikum faismaAAooni
36:25Lo! I have believed in your Lord, so hear me
قِيلَ ٱدۡخُلِ ٱلۡجَنَّةَۖ قَالَ يَٰلَيۡتَ قَوۡمِي يَعۡلَمُونَ٢٦
Qeela odkhuli aljannata qala ya layta qawmee yaAAlamoona
36:26It was said (unto him): Enter paradise. He said: Would that my people knew
بِمَا غَفَرَ لِي رَبِّي وَجَعَلَنِي مِنَ ٱلۡمُكۡرَمِينَ٢٧
Bima ghafara lee rabbee wajaAAalanee mina almukrameena
36:27With what (munificence) my Lord hath pardoned me and made me of the honoured ones
۞وَمَآ أَنزَلۡنَا عَلَىٰ قَوۡمِهِۦ مِنۢ بَعۡدِهِۦ مِن جُندٖ مِّنَ ٱلسَّمَآءِ وَمَا كُنَّا مُنزِلِينَ٢٨
Wama anzalna AAala qawmihi min baAAdihi min jundin mina alssamai wama kunna munzileena
36:28We sent not down against his people after him a host from heaven, nor do We ever send
إِن كَانَتۡ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ فَإِذَا هُمۡ خَٰمِدُونَ٢٩
In kanat illa sayhatan wahidatan faitha hum khamidoona
36:29It was but one Shout, and lo! they were extinct
يَٰحَسۡرَةً عَلَى ٱلۡعِبَادِۚ مَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ٣٠
Ya hasratan AAala alAAibadi ma yateehim min rasoolin illa kanoo bihi yastahzioona
36:30Ah, the anguish for the bondmen! Never came there unto them a messenger but they did mock him
أَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ كَمۡ أَهۡلَكۡنَا قَبۡلَهُم مِّنَ ٱلۡقُرُونِ أَنَّهُمۡ إِلَيۡهِمۡ لَا يَرۡجِعُونَ٣١
Alam yaraw kam ahlakna qablahum mina alqurooni annahum ilayhim la yarjiAAoona
36:31Have they not seen how many generations We destroyed before them, which indeed returned not unto them
وَإِن كُلّٞ لَّمَّا جَمِيعٞ لَّدَيۡنَا مُحۡضَرُونَ٣٢
Wain kullun lamma jameeAAun ladayna muhdaroona
36:32But all, without exception, will be brought before Us
وَءَايَةٞ لَّهُمُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ ٱلۡمَيۡتَةُ أَحۡيَيۡنَٰهَا وَأَخۡرَجۡنَا مِنۡهَا حَبّٗا فَمِنۡهُ يَأۡكُلُونَ٣٣
Waayatun lahumu alardu almaytatu ahyaynaha waakhrajna minha habban faminhu yakuloona
36:33A token unto them is the dead earth. We revive it, and We bring forth from it grain so that they eat thereof
وَجَعَلۡنَا فِيهَا جَنَّـٰتٖ مِّن نَّخِيلٖ وَأَعۡنَٰبٖ وَفَجَّرۡنَا فِيهَا مِنَ ٱلۡعُيُونِ٣٤
WajaAAalna feeha jannatin min nakheelin waaAAnabin wafajjarna feeha mina alAAuyooni
36:34And We have placed therein gardens of the date-palm and grapes, and We have caused springs of water to gush forth therein
لِيَأۡكُلُواْ مِن ثَمَرِهِۦ وَمَا عَمِلَتۡهُ أَيۡدِيهِمۡۚ أَفَلَا يَشۡكُرُونَ٣٥
Liyakuloo min thamarihi wama AAamilathu aydeehim afala yashkuroona
36:35That they may eat of the fruit thereof, and their hands made it not. Will they not, then, give thanks
سُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلۡأَزۡوَٰجَ كُلَّهَا مِمَّا تُنۢبِتُ ٱلۡأَرۡضُ وَمِنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡ وَمِمَّا لَا يَعۡلَمُونَ٣٦
Subhana allathee khalaqa alazwaja kullaha mimma tunbitu alardu wamin anfusihim wamimma la yaAAlamoona
36:36Glory be to Him Who created all the sexual pairs, of that which the earth groweth, and of themselves, and of that which they know not
وَءَايَةٞ لَّهُمُ ٱلَّيۡلُ نَسۡلَخُ مِنۡهُ ٱلنَّهَارَ فَإِذَا هُم مُّظۡلِمُونَ٣٧
Waayatun lahumu allaylu naslakhu minhu alnnahara faitha hum muthlimoona
36:37A token unto them is night. We strip it of the day, and lo! they are in darkness
وَٱلشَّمۡسُ تَجۡرِي لِمُسۡتَقَرّٖ لَّهَاۚ ذَٰلِكَ تَقۡدِيرُ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ٣٨
Waalshshamsu tajree limustaqarrin laha thalika taqdeeru alAAazeezi alAAaleemi
36:38And the sun runneth on unto a resting-place for him. That is the measuring of the Mighty, the Wise
وَٱلۡقَمَرَ قَدَّرۡنَٰهُ مَنَازِلَ حَتَّىٰ عَادَ كَٱلۡعُرۡجُونِ ٱلۡقَدِيمِ٣٩
Waalqamara qaddarnahu manazila hatta AAada kaalAAurjooni alqadeemi
36:39And for the moon We have appointed mansions till she return like an old shrivelled palm-leaf
لَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ يَنۢبَغِي لَهَآ أَن تُدۡرِكَ ٱلۡقَمَرَ وَلَا ٱلَّيۡلُ سَابِقُ ٱلنَّهَارِۚ وَكُلّٞ فِي فَلَكٖ يَسۡبَحُونَ٤٠
La alshshamsu yanbaghee laha an tudrika alqamara wala allaylu sabiqu alnnahari wakullun fee falakin yasbahoona
36:40It is not for the sun to overtake the moon, nor doth the night outstrip the day. They float each in an orbit
وَءَايَةٞ لَّهُمۡ أَنَّا حَمَلۡنَا ذُرِّيَّتَهُمۡ فِي ٱلۡفُلۡكِ ٱلۡمَشۡحُونِ٤١
Waayatun lahum anna hamalna thurriyyatahum fee alfulki almashhooni
36:41And a token unto them is that We bear their offspring in the laden ship
وَخَلَقۡنَا لَهُم مِّن مِّثۡلِهِۦ مَا يَرۡكَبُونَ٤٢

36:42And have created for them of the like thereof whereon they ride
وَإِن نَّشَأۡ نُغۡرِقۡهُمۡ فَلَا صَرِيخَ لَهُمۡ وَلَا هُمۡ يُنقَذُونَ٤٣
Wain nasha nughriqhum fala sareekha lahum wala hum yunqathoona
36:43And if We will, We drown them, and there is no help for them, neither can they be saved
إِلَّا رَحۡمَةٗ مِّنَّا وَمَتَٰعًا إِلَىٰ حِينٖ٤٤

36:44Unless by mercy from Us and as comfort for a while
وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمُ ٱتَّقُواْ مَا بَيۡنَ أَيۡدِيكُمۡ وَمَا خَلۡفَكُمۡ لَعَلَّكُمۡ تُرۡحَمُونَ٤٥

36:45When it is said unto them: Beware of that which is before you and that which is behind you, that haply ye may find mercy (they are heedless)

Wama tateehim min ayatin min ayati rabbihim illa kanoo AAanha muAArideena
36:46Never came a token of the tokens of their Lord to them, but they did turn away from it
وَإِذَا قِيلَ لَهُمۡ أَنفِقُواْ مِمَّا رَزَقَكُمُ ٱللَّهُ قَالَ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ لِلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُوٓاْ أَنُطۡعِمُ مَن لَّوۡ يَشَآءُ ٱللَّهُ أَطۡعَمَهُۥٓ إِنۡ أَنتُمۡ إِلَّا فِي ضَلَٰلٖ مُّبِينٖ٤٧
Waitha qeela lahum anfiqoo mimma razaqakumu Allahu qala allatheena kafaroo lillatheena amanoo anutAAimu man law yashao Allahu atAAamahu in antum illa fee dalalin mubeenin
36:47And when it is said unto them: Spend of that wherewith Allah hath provided you, those who disbelieve say unto those who believe: Shall we feed those whom Allah, if He willed, would feed? Ye are in naught else than error manifest
وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ٤٨
Wayaqooloona mata hatha alwaAAdu in kuntum sadiqeena

مَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ تَأۡخُذُهُمۡ وَهُمۡ يَخِصِّمُونَ٤٩
Ma yanthuroona illa sayhatan wahidatan takhuthuhum wahum yakhissimoona
36:49They await but one Shout, which will surprise them while they are disputing
فَلَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ تَوۡصِيَةٗ وَلَآ إِلَىٰٓ أَهۡلِهِمۡ يَرۡجِعُونَ٥٠

وَنُفِخَ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَإِذَا هُم مِّنَ ٱلۡأَجۡدَاثِ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِمۡ يَنسِلُونَ٥١
Wanufikha fee alssoori faitha hum mina alajdathi ila rabbihim yansiloona
36:51And the trumpet is blown and lo! from the graves they hie unto their Lord
قَالُواْ يَٰوَيۡلَنَا مَنۢ بَعَثَنَا مِن مَّرۡقَدِنَاۜۗ هَٰذَا مَا وَعَدَ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَصَدَقَ ٱلۡمُرۡسَلُونَ٥٢
Qaloo ya waylana man baAAathana min marqadina hatha ma waAAada alrrahmanu wasadaqa almursaloona
36:52Crying: Woe upon us! Who hath raised us from our place of sleep? This is that which the Beneficent did promise, and the messengers spoke truth
إِن كَانَتۡ إِلَّا صَيۡحَةٗ وَٰحِدَةٗ فَإِذَا هُمۡ جَمِيعٞ لَّدَيۡنَا مُحۡضَرُونَ٥٣
In kanat illa sayhatan wahidatan faitha hum jameeAAun ladayna muhdaroona
36:53It is but one Shout, and behold them brought together before Us
فَٱلۡيَوۡمَ لَا تُظۡلَمُ نَفۡسٞ شَيۡـٔٗا وَلَا تُجۡزَوۡنَ إِلَّا مَا كُنتُمۡ تَعۡمَلُونَ٥٤
Faalyawma la tuthlamu nafsun shayan wala tujzawna illa ma kuntum taAAmaloona
36:54This day no soul is wronged in aught; nor are ye requited aught save what ye used to do
إِنَّ أَصۡحَٰبَ ٱلۡجَنَّةِ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ فِي شُغُلٖ فَٰكِهُونَ٥٥
Inna ashaba aljannati alyawma fee shughulin fakihoona
36:55Lo! those who merit paradise this day are happily employed
هُمۡ وَأَزۡوَٰجُهُمۡ فِي ظِلَٰلٍ عَلَى ٱلۡأَرَآئِكِ مُتَّكِـُٔونَ٥٦
Hum waazwajuhum fee thilalin AAala alaraiki muttakioona
36:56They and their wives, in pleasant shade, on thrones reclining
لَهُمۡ فِيهَا فَٰكِهَةٞ وَلَهُم مَّا يَدَّعُونَ٥٧
Lahum feeha fakihatun walahum ma yaddaAAoona
36:57Theirs the fruit (of their good deeds) and theirs (all) that they ask
سَلَٰمٞ قَوۡلٗا مِّن رَّبّٖ رَّحِيمٖ٥٨
Salamun qawlan min rabbin raheemin
36:58The word from a Merciful Lord (for them) is: Peace
وَٱمۡتَٰزُواْ ٱلۡيَوۡمَ أَيُّهَا ٱلۡمُجۡرِمُونَ٥٩
Waimtazoo alyawma ayyuha almujrimoona
36:59But avaunt ye, O ye guilty, this day
۞أَلَمۡ أَعۡهَدۡ إِلَيۡكُمۡ يَٰبَنِيٓ ءَادَمَ أَن لَّا تَعۡبُدُواْ ٱلشَّيۡطَٰنَۖ إِنَّهُۥ لَكُمۡ عَدُوّٞ مُّبِينٞ٦٠
Alam aAAhad ilaykum ya banee adama an la taAAbudoo alshshaytana innahu lakum AAaduwwun mubeenun
36:60Did I not charge you, O ye sons of Adam, that ye worship not the devil - Lo! he is your open foe
وَأَنِ ٱعۡبُدُونِيۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ٦١
Waani oAAbudoonee hatha siratun mustaqeemun
36:61But that ye worship Me? That was the right path
وَلَقَدۡ أَضَلَّ مِنكُمۡ جِبِلّٗا كَثِيرًاۖ أَفَلَمۡ تَكُونُواْ تَعۡقِلُونَ٦٢
Walaqad adalla minkum jibillan katheeran afalam takoonoo taAAqiloona
36:62Yet he hath led astray of you a great multitude. Had ye then no sense
هَٰذِهِۦ جَهَنَّمُ ٱلَّتِي كُنتُمۡ تُوعَدُونَ٦٣
Hathihi jahannamu allatee kuntum tooAAadoona
36:63This is hell which ye were promised (if ye followed him)
ٱصۡلَوۡهَا ٱلۡيَوۡمَ بِمَا كُنتُمۡ تَكۡفُرُونَ٦٤
Islawha alyawma bima kuntum takfuroona
36:64Burn therein this day for that ye disbelieved
ٱلۡيَوۡمَ نَخۡتِمُ عَلَىٰٓ أَفۡوَٰهِهِمۡ وَتُكَلِّمُنَآ أَيۡدِيهِمۡ وَتَشۡهَدُ أَرۡجُلُهُم بِمَا كَانُواْ يَكۡسِبُونَ٦٥
Alyawma nakhtimu AAala afwahihim watukallimuna aydeehim watashhadu arjuluhum bima kanoo yaksiboona
36:65This day We seal up their mouths, and their hands speak out to Us and their feet bear witness as to what they used to earn
وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَطَمَسۡنَا عَلَىٰٓ أَعۡيُنِهِمۡ فَٱسۡتَبَقُواْ ٱلصِّرَٰطَ فَأَنَّىٰ يُبۡصِرُونَ٦٦
Walaw nashao latamasna AAala aAAyunihim faistabaqoo alssirata faanna yubsiroona
36:66And had We willed, We verily could have quenched their eyesight so that they should struggle for the way. Then how could they have seen
وَلَوۡ نَشَآءُ لَمَسَخۡنَٰهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَكَانَتِهِمۡ فَمَا ٱسۡتَطَٰعُواْ مُضِيّٗا وَلَا يَرۡجِعُونَ٦٧
Walaw nashao lamasakhnahum AAala makanatihim fama istataAAoo mudiyyan wala yarjiAAoona
36:67And had We willed, We verily could have fixed them in their place, making them powerless to go forward or turn back
وَمَن نُّعَمِّرۡهُ نُنَكِّسۡهُ فِي ٱلۡخَلۡقِۚ أَفَلَا يَعۡقِلُونَ٦٨
Waman nuAAammirhu nunakkishu fee alkhalqi afala yaAAqiloona
36:68He whom we bring unto old age, We reverse him in creation (making him go back to weakness after strength). Have ye then no sense
وَمَا عَلَّمۡنَٰهُ ٱلشِّعۡرَ وَمَا يَنۢبَغِي لَهُۥٓۚ إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ وَقُرۡءَانٞ مُّبِينٞ٦٩
Wama AAallamnahu alshshiAAra wama yanbaghee lahu in huwa illa thikrun waquranun mubeenun
36:69And We have not taught him (Muhammad) poetry, nor is it meet for him. This is naught else than a Reminder and a Lecture making plain
لِّيُنذِرَ مَن كَانَ حَيّٗا وَيَحِقَّ ٱلۡقَوۡلُ عَلَى ٱلۡكَٰفِرِينَ٧٠
Liyunthira man kana hayyan wayahiqqa alqawlu AAala alkafireena
36:70To warn whosoever liveth, and that the word may be fulfilled against the disbelievers
أَوَلَمۡ يَرَوۡاْ أَنَّا خَلَقۡنَا لَهُم مِّمَّا عَمِلَتۡ أَيۡدِينَآ أَنۡعَٰمٗا فَهُمۡ لَهَا مَٰلِكُونَ٧١
Awalam yaraw anna khalaqna lahum mimma AAamilat aydeena anAAaman fahum laha malikoona
36:71Have they not seen how We have created for them of Our handiwork the cattle, so that they are their owners
وَذَلَّلۡنَٰهَا لَهُمۡ فَمِنۡهَا رَكُوبُهُمۡ وَمِنۡهَا يَأۡكُلُونَ٧٢
Wathallalnaha lahum faminha rakoobuhum waminha yakuloona
36:72And have subdued them unto them, so that some of them they have for riding, some for food
وَلَهُمۡ فِيهَا مَنَٰفِعُ وَمَشَارِبُۚ أَفَلَا يَشۡكُرُونَ٧٣
Walahum feeha manafiAAu wamasharibu afala yashkuroona
36:73Benefits and (divers) drinks have they from them. Will they not then give thanks
وَٱتَّخَذُواْ مِن دُونِ ٱللَّهِ ءَالِهَةٗ لَّعَلَّهُمۡ يُنصَرُونَ٧٤
Waittakhathoo min dooni Allahi alihatan laAAallahum yunsaroona
36:74And they have taken (other) gods beside Allah, in order that they may be helped
لَا يَسۡتَطِيعُونَ نَصۡرَهُمۡ وَهُمۡ لَهُمۡ جُندٞ مُّحۡضَرُونَ٧٥
La yastateeAAoona nasrahum wahum lahum jundun muhdaroona
36:75It is not in their power to help them; but they (the worshippers) are unto them a host in arms
فَلَا يَحۡزُنكَ قَوۡلُهُمۡۘ إِنَّا نَعۡلَمُ مَا يُسِرُّونَ وَمَا يُعۡلِنُونَ٧٦
Fala yahzunka qawluhum inna naAAlamu ma yusirroona wama yuAAlinoona
36:76So let not their speech grieve thee (O Muhammad). Lo! We know what they conceal and what proclaim
أَوَلَمۡ يَرَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ أَنَّا خَلَقۡنَٰهُ مِن نُّطۡفَةٖ فَإِذَا هُوَ خَصِيمٞ مُّبِينٞ٧٧
Awalam yara alinsanu anna khalaqnahu min nutfatin faitha huwa khaseemun mubeenun
36:77Hath not man seen that We have created him from a drop of seed? Yet lo! he is an open opponent
وَضَرَبَ لَنَا مَثَلٗا وَنَسِيَ خَلۡقَهُۥۖ قَالَ مَن يُحۡيِ ٱلۡعِظَٰمَ وَهِيَ رَمِيمٞ٧٨
Wadaraba lana mathalan wanasiya khalqahu qala man yuhyee alAAithama wahiya rameemun
36:78And he hath coined for Us a similitude, and hath forgotten the fact of his creation, saying: Who will revive these bones when they have rotted away
قُلۡ يُحۡيِيهَا ٱلَّذِيٓ أَنشَأَهَآ أَوَّلَ مَرَّةٖۖ وَهُوَ بِكُلِّ خَلۡقٍ عَلِيمٌ٧٩
Qul yuhyeeha allathee anshaaha awwala marratin wahuwa bikulli khalqin AAaleemun
36:79Say: He will revive them Who produced them at the first, for He is Knower of every creation
ٱلَّذِي جَعَلَ لَكُم مِّنَ ٱلشَّجَرِ ٱلۡأَخۡضَرِ نَارٗا فَإِذَآ أَنتُم مِّنۡهُ تُوقِدُونَ٨٠
Allathee jaAAala lakum mina alshshajari alakhdari naran faitha antum minhu tooqidoona
36:80Who hath appointed for you fire from the green tree, and behold! ye kindle from it
أَوَلَيۡسَ ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ بِقَٰدِرٍ عَلَىٰٓ أَن يَخۡلُقَ مِثۡلَهُمۚ بَلَىٰ وَهُوَ ٱلۡخَلَّـٰقُ ٱلۡعَلِيمُ٨١
Awalaysa allathee khalaqa alssamawati waalarda biqadirin AAala an yakhluqa mithlahum bala wahuwa alkhallaqu alAAaleemu
36:81Is not He Who created the heavens and the earth Able to create the like of them? Aye, that He is! for He is the All-Wise Creator
إِنَّمَآ أَمۡرُهُۥٓ إِذَآ أَرَادَ شَيۡـًٔا أَن يَقُولَ لَهُۥ كُن فَيَكُونُ٨٢
Innama amruhu itha arada shayan an yaqoola lahu kun fayakoonu
36:82But His command, when He intendeth a thing, is only that He saith unto it: Be! and it is
فَسُبۡحَٰنَ ٱلَّذِي بِيَدِهِۦ مَلَكُوتُ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُرۡجَعُونَ٨٣
Fasubhana allathee biyadihi malakootu kulli shayin wailayhi turjaAAoona
36:83Therefor Glory be to Him in Whose hand is the dominion over all things! Unto Him ye will be brought back
