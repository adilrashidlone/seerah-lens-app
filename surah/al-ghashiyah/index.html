سورة الغاشية88. Al-Ghashiyah · The Overwhelming
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلۡغَٰشِيَةِ١
Hal ataka hadeethu alghashiyati
88:1Hath there come unto thee tidings of the Overwhelming
وُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٍ خَٰشِعَةٌ٢
Wujoohun yawmaithin khashiAAatun
88:2On that day (many) faces will be downcast
عَامِلَةٞ نَّاصِبَةٞ٣
AAamilatun nasibatun
88:3Toiling, weary
تَصۡلَىٰ نَارًا حَامِيَةٗ٤
Tasla naran hamiyatan
88:4Scorched by burning fire
تُسۡقَىٰ مِنۡ عَيۡنٍ ءَانِيَةٖ٥
Tusqa min AAaynin aniyatin
88:5Drinking from a boiling spring
لَّيۡسَ لَهُمۡ طَعَامٌ إِلَّا مِن ضَرِيعٖ٦
Laysa lahum taAAamun illa min dareeAAin
88:6No food for them save bitter thorn-fruit
لَّا يُسۡمِنُ وَلَا يُغۡنِي مِن جُوعٖ٧
La yusminu wala yughnee min jooAAin
88:7Which doth not nourish nor release from hunger
وُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ نَّاعِمَةٞ٨
Wujoohun yawmaithin naAAimatun
88:8In that day other faces will be calm
لِّسَعۡيِهَا رَاضِيَةٞ٩
LisaAAyiha radiyatun
88:9Glad for their effort past
فِي جَنَّةٍ عَالِيَةٖ١٠
Fee jannatin AAaliyatin
88:10In a high Garden
لَّا تَسۡمَعُ فِيهَا لَٰغِيَةٗ١١
La tasmaAAu feeha laghiyatan
88:11Where they hear no idle speech
فِيهَا عَيۡنٞ جَارِيَةٞ١٢
Feeha AAaynun jariyatun
88:12Wherein is a gushing spring
فِيهَا سُرُرٞ مَّرۡفُوعَةٞ١٣
Feeha sururun marfooAAatun
88:13Wherein are couches raised
وَأَكۡوَابٞ مَّوۡضُوعَةٞ١٤
Waakwabun mawdooAAatun
88:14And goblets set at hand
وَنَمَارِقُ مَصۡفُوفَةٞ١٥
Wanamariqu masfoofatun
88:15And cushions ranged
وَزَرَابِيُّ مَبۡثُوثَةٌ١٦
Wazarabiyyu mabthoothatun
88:16And silken carpets spread
أَفَلَا يَنظُرُونَ إِلَى ٱلۡإِبِلِ كَيۡفَ خُلِقَتۡ١٧
Afala yanthuroona ila alibili kayfa khuliqat
88:17Will they not regard the camels, how they are created
وَإِلَى ٱلسَّمَآءِ كَيۡفَ رُفِعَتۡ١٨
Waila alssamai kayfa rufiAAat
88:18And the heaven, how it is raised
وَإِلَى ٱلۡجِبَالِ كَيۡفَ نُصِبَتۡ١٩
Waila aljibali kayfa nusibat
88:19And the hills, how they are set up
وَإِلَى ٱلۡأَرۡضِ كَيۡفَ سُطِحَتۡ٢٠
Waila alardi kayfa sutihat
88:20And the earth, how it is spread
فَذَكِّرۡ إِنَّمَآ أَنتَ مُذَكِّرٞ٢١
Fathakkir innama anta muthakkirun
88:21Remind them, for thou art but a remembrancer
لَّسۡتَ عَلَيۡهِم بِمُصَيۡطِرٍ٢٢
Lasta AAalayhim bimusaytirin
88:22Thou art not at all a warder over them
إِلَّا مَن تَوَلَّىٰ وَكَفَرَ٢٣
Illa man tawalla wakafara
88:23But whoso is averse and disbelieveth
فَيُعَذِّبُهُ ٱللَّهُ ٱلۡعَذَابَ ٱلۡأَكۡبَرَ٢٤
FayuAAaththibuhu Allahu alAAathaba alakbara
88:24Allah will punish him with direst punishment
إِنَّ إِلَيۡنَآ إِيَابَهُمۡ٢٥
Inna ilayna iyabahum
88:25Lo! unto Us is their return
ثُمَّ إِنَّ عَلَيۡنَا حِسَابَهُم٢٦
Thumma inna AAalayna hisabahum
88:26And Ours their reckoning
