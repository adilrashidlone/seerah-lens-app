سورة الإنفطار82. Al-Infitar · The Cleaving
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
إِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ ٱنفَطَرَتۡ١
Itha alssamao infatarat
82:1When the heaven is cleft asunder
وَإِذَا ٱلۡكَوَاكِبُ ٱنتَثَرَتۡ٢
Waitha alkawakibu intatharat
82:2When the planets are dispersed
وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ فُجِّرَتۡ٣
Waitha albiharu fujjirat
82:3When the seas are poured forth
وَإِذَا ٱلۡقُبُورُ بُعۡثِرَتۡ٤
Waitha alqubooru buAAthirat
82:4And the sepulchres are overturned
عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسٞ مَّا قَدَّمَتۡ وَأَخَّرَتۡ٥
AAalimat nafsun ma qaddamat waakhkharat
82:5A soul will know what it hath sent before (it) and what left behind
يَـٰٓأَيُّهَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَا غَرَّكَ بِرَبِّكَ ٱلۡكَرِيمِ٦
Ya ayyuha alinsanu ma gharraka birabbika alkareemi
82:6O man! What hath made thee careless concerning thy Lord, the Bountiful
ٱلَّذِي خَلَقَكَ فَسَوَّىٰكَ فَعَدَلَكَ٧
Allathee khalaqaka fasawwaka faAAadalaka
82:7Who created thee, then fashioned, then proportioned thee
فِيٓ أَيِّ صُورَةٖ مَّا شَآءَ رَكَّبَكَ٨
Fee ayyi sooratin ma shaa rakkabaka
82:8Into whatsoever form He will, He casteth thee
كَلَّا بَلۡ تُكَذِّبُونَ بِٱلدِّينِ٩
Kalla bal tukaththiboona bialddeeni
82:9Nay, but ye deny the Judgment
وَإِنَّ عَلَيۡكُمۡ لَحَٰفِظِينَ١٠
Wainna AAalaykum lahafitheena
82:10Lo! there are above you guardians
كِرَامٗا كَٰتِبِينَ١١
Kiraman katibeena
82:11Generous and recording
يَعۡلَمُونَ مَا تَفۡعَلُونَ١٢
YaAAlamoona ma tafAAaloona
82:12Who know (all) that ye do
إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٖ١٣
Inna alabrara lafee naAAeemin
82:13Lo! the righteous verily will be in delight
وَإِنَّ ٱلۡفُجَّارَ لَفِي جَحِيمٖ١٤
Wainna alfujjara lafee jaheemin
82:14And lo! the wicked verily will be in hell
يَصۡلَوۡنَهَا يَوۡمَ ٱلدِّينِ١٥
Yaslawnaha yawma alddeeni
82:15They will burn therein on the Day of Judgment
وَمَا هُمۡ عَنۡهَا بِغَآئِبِينَ١٦
Wama hum AAanha bighaibeena
82:16And will not be absent thence
وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ١٧
Wama adraka ma yawmu alddeeni
82:17Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Day of Judgment is
ثُمَّ مَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ١٨
Thumma ma adraka ma yawmu alddeeni
82:18Again, what will convey unto thee what the Day of Judgment is
يَوۡمَ لَا تَمۡلِكُ نَفۡسٞ لِّنَفۡسٖ شَيۡـٔٗاۖ وَٱلۡأَمۡرُ يَوۡمَئِذٖ لِّلَّهِ١٩
Yawma la tamliku nafsun linafsin shayan waalamru yawmaithin lillahi
82:19A day on which no soul hath power at all for any (other) soul. The (absolute) command on that day is Allah's
