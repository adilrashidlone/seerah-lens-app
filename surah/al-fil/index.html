سورة الفيل105. Al-Fil · The Elephant
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
أَلَمۡ تَرَ كَيۡفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصۡحَٰبِ ٱلۡفِيلِ١
Alam tara kayfa faAAala rabbuka biashabi alfeeli
105:1Hast thou not seen how thy Lord dealt with the owners of the Elephant
أَلَمۡ يَجۡعَلۡ كَيۡدَهُمۡ فِي تَضۡلِيلٖ٢
Alam yajAAal kaydahum fee tadleelin
105:2Did He not bring their stratagem to naught
وَأَرۡسَلَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ طَيۡرًا أَبَابِيلَ٣
Waarsala AAalayhim tayran ababeela
105:3And send against them swarms of flying creatures
تَرۡمِيهِم بِحِجَارَةٖ مِّن سِجِّيلٖ٤
Tarmeehim bihijaratin min sijjeelin
105:4Which pelted them with stones of baked clay
فَجَعَلَهُمۡ كَعَصۡفٖ مَّأۡكُولِۭ٥
FajaAAalahum kaAAasfin makoolin
105:5And made them like green crops devoured (by cattle)
