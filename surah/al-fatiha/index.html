Surah Al-Fatihah: Arabic, Transliteration and English Meaning

Surah Al-Fatihah (الفاتحة, “The Opener”) is surah 1 of the Quran, a Makki surah of 7 ayat. Read it below in Arabic with transliteration and the English meaning.

سورة الفاتحة1. Al-Fatihah · The Opener
بِسۡمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ١
Bismi Allahi alrrahmani alrraheemi
1:1In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful
ٱلۡحَمۡدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ٢
Alhamdu lillahi rabbi alAAalameena
1:2Praise be to Allah, Lord of the Worlds
ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ٣
Alrrahmani alrraheemi
1:3The Beneficent, the Merciful
مَٰلِكِ يَوۡمِ ٱلدِّينِ٤
Maliki yawmi alddeeni
1:4Master of the Day of Judgment
إِيَّاكَ نَعۡبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسۡتَعِينُ٥
Iyyaka naAAbudu waiyyaka nastaAAeenu
1:5Thee (alone) we worship; Thee (alone) we ask for help
ٱهۡدِنَا ٱلصِّرَٰطَ ٱلۡمُسۡتَقِيمَ٦
Ihdina alssirata almustaqeema
1:6Show us the straight path
صِرَٰطَ ٱلَّذِينَ أَنۡعَمۡتَ عَلَيۡهِمۡ غَيۡرِ ٱلۡمَغۡضُوبِ عَلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَا ٱلضَّآلِّينَ٧
Sirata allatheena anAAamta AAalayhim ghayri almaghdoobi AAalayhim wala alddalleena
1:7The path of those whom Thou hast favoured; Not the (path) of those who earn Thine anger nor of those who go astray
