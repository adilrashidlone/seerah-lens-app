سورة النبإ78. An-Naba · The Tidings
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
عَمَّ يَتَسَآءَلُونَ١
AAamma yatasaaloona
78:1Whereof do they question one another
عَنِ ٱلنَّبَإِ ٱلۡعَظِيمِ٢
AAani alnnabai alAAatheemi
78:2(It is) of the awful tidings
ٱلَّذِي هُمۡ فِيهِ مُخۡتَلِفُونَ٣
Allathee hum feehi mukhtalifoona
78:3Concerning which they are in disagreement
كَلَّا سَيَعۡلَمُونَ٤
Kalla sayaAAlamoona
78:4Nay, but they will come to know
ثُمَّ كَلَّا سَيَعۡلَمُونَ٥
Thumma kalla sayaAAlamoona
78:5Nay, again, but they will come to know
أَلَمۡ نَجۡعَلِ ٱلۡأَرۡضَ مِهَٰدٗا٦
Alam najAAali alarda mihadan
78:6Have We not made the earth an expanse
وَٱلۡجِبَالَ أَوۡتَادٗا٧
Waaljibala awtadan
78:7And the high hills bulwarks
وَخَلَقۡنَٰكُمۡ أَزۡوَٰجٗا٨
Wakhalaqnakum azwajan
78:8And We have created you in pairs
وَجَعَلۡنَا نَوۡمَكُمۡ سُبَاتٗا٩
WajaAAalna nawmakum subatan
78:9And have appointed your sleep for repose
وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلَّيۡلَ لِبَاسٗا١٠
WajaAAalna allayla libasan
78:10And have appointed the night as a cloak
وَجَعَلۡنَا ٱلنَّهَارَ مَعَاشٗا١١
WajaAAalna alnnahara maAAashan
78:11And have appointed the day for livelihood
وَبَنَيۡنَا فَوۡقَكُمۡ سَبۡعٗا شِدَادٗا١٢
Wabanayna fawqakum sabAAan shidadan
78:12And We have built above you seven strong (heavens)
وَجَعَلۡنَا سِرَاجٗا وَهَّاجٗا١٣
WajaAAalna sirajan wahhajan
78:13And have appointed a dazzling lamp
وَأَنزَلۡنَا مِنَ ٱلۡمُعۡصِرَٰتِ مَآءٗ ثَجَّاجٗا١٤
Waanzalna mina almuAAsirati maan thajjajan
78:14And have sent down from the rainy clouds abundant water
لِّنُخۡرِجَ بِهِۦ حَبّٗا وَنَبَاتٗا١٥
Linukhrija bihi habban wanabatan
78:15Thereby to produce grain and plant
وَجَنَّـٰتٍ أَلۡفَافًا١٦
Wajannatin alfafan
78:16And gardens of thick foliage
إِنَّ يَوۡمَ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ كَانَ مِيقَٰتٗا١٧
Inna yawma alfasli kana meeqatan
78:17Lo! the Day of Decision is a fixed time
يَوۡمَ يُنفَخُ فِي ٱلصُّورِ فَتَأۡتُونَ أَفۡوَاجٗا١٨
Yawma yunfakhu fee alssoori fatatoona afwajan
78:18A day when the trumpet is blown and ye come in multitudes
وَفُتِحَتِ ٱلسَّمَآءُ فَكَانَتۡ أَبۡوَٰبٗا١٩
Wafutihati alssamao fakanat abwaban
78:19And the heaven is opened and becometh as gates
وَسُيِّرَتِ ٱلۡجِبَالُ فَكَانَتۡ سَرَابًا٢٠
Wasuyyirati aljibalu fakanat saraban
78:20And the hills are set in motion and become as a mirage
إِنَّ جَهَنَّمَ كَانَتۡ مِرۡصَادٗا٢١
Inna jahannama kanat mirsadan
78:21Lo! hell lurketh in ambush
لِّلطَّـٰغِينَ مَـَٔابٗا٢٢
Lilttagheena maaban
78:22A home for the rebellious
لَّـٰبِثِينَ فِيهَآ أَحۡقَابٗا٢٣
Labitheena feeha ahqaban
78:23They will abide therein for ages
لَّا يَذُوقُونَ فِيهَا بَرۡدٗا وَلَا شَرَابًا٢٤
La yathooqoona feeha bardan wala sharaban
78:24Therein taste they neither coolness nor (any) drink
إِلَّا حَمِيمٗا وَغَسَّاقٗا٢٥
Illa hameeman waghassaqan
78:25Save boiling water and a paralysing cold
جَزَآءٗ وِفَاقًا٢٦
Jazaan wifaqan
78:26Reward proportioned (to their evil deeds)
إِنَّهُمۡ كَانُواْ لَا يَرۡجُونَ حِسَابٗا٢٧
Innahum kanoo la yarjoona hisaban
78:27For lo! they looked not for a reckoning
وَكَذَّبُواْ بِـَٔايَٰتِنَا كِذَّابٗا٢٨
Wakaththaboo biayatina kiththaban
78:28They called Our revelations false with strong denial
وَكُلَّ شَيۡءٍ أَحۡصَيۡنَٰهُ كِتَٰبٗا٢٩
Wakulla shayin ahsaynahu kitaban
78:29Everything have We recorded in a Book
فَذُوقُواْ فَلَن نَّزِيدَكُمۡ إِلَّا عَذَابًا٣٠
Fathooqoo falan nazeedakum illa AAathaban
78:30So taste (of that which ye have earned). No increase do We give you save of torment
إِنَّ لِلۡمُتَّقِينَ مَفَازًا٣١
Inna lilmuttaqeena mafazan
78:31Lo! for the duteous is achievement
حَدَآئِقَ وَأَعۡنَٰبٗا٣٢
Hadaiqa waaAAnaban
78:32Gardens enclosed and vineyards
وَكَوَاعِبَ أَتۡرَابٗا٣٣
WakawaAAiba atraban
78:33And voluptuous women of equal age
وَكَأۡسٗا دِهَاقٗا٣٤
Wakasan dihaqan
78:34And a full cup
لَّا يَسۡمَعُونَ فِيهَا لَغۡوٗا وَلَا كِذَّـٰبٗا٣٥
La yasmaAAoona feeha laghwan wala kiththaban
78:35There hear they never vain discourse, nor lying
جَزَآءٗ مِّن رَّبِّكَ عَطَآءً حِسَابٗا٣٦
Jazaan min rabbika AAataan hisaban
78:36Requital from thy Lord - a gift in payment
رَّبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنِۖ لَا يَمۡلِكُونَ مِنۡهُ خِطَابٗا٣٧
Rabbi alssamawati waalardi wama baynahuma alrrahmani la yamlikoona minhu khitaban
78:37Lord of the heavens and the earth, and (all) that is between them, the Beneficent; with Whom none can converse
يَوۡمَ يَقُومُ ٱلرُّوحُ وَٱلۡمَلَـٰٓئِكَةُ صَفّٗاۖ لَّا يَتَكَلَّمُونَ إِلَّا مَنۡ أَذِنَ لَهُ ٱلرَّحۡمَٰنُ وَقَالَ صَوَابٗا٣٨
Yawma yaqoomu alrroohu waalmalaikatu saffan la yatakallamoona illa man athina lahu alrrahmanu waqala sawaban
78:38On the day when the angels and the Spirit stand arrayed, they speak not, saving him whom the Beneficent alloweth and who speaketh right
ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡيَوۡمُ ٱلۡحَقُّۖ فَمَن شَآءَ ٱتَّخَذَ إِلَىٰ رَبِّهِۦ مَـَٔابًا٣٩
Thalika alyawmu alhaqqu faman shaa ittakhatha ila rabbihi maaban
78:39That is the True Day. So whoso will should seek recourse unto his Lord
إِنَّآ أَنذَرۡنَٰكُمۡ عَذَابٗا قَرِيبٗا يَوۡمَ يَنظُرُ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مَا قَدَّمَتۡ يَدَاهُ وَيَقُولُ ٱلۡكَافِرُ يَٰلَيۡتَنِي كُنتُ تُرَٰبَۢا٤٠
Inna antharnakum AAathaban qareeban yawma yanthuru almaro ma qaddamat yadahu wayaqoolu alkafiru ya laytanee kuntu turaban
78:40Lo! We warn you of a doom at hand, a day whereon a man will look on that which his own hands have sent before, and the disbeliever will cry: "Would that I were dust
