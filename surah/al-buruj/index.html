سورة البروج85. Al-Buruj · The Mansions of the Stars
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلسَّمَآءِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡبُرُوجِ١
Waalssamai thati alburooji
85:1By the heaven, holding mansions of the stars
وَٱلۡيَوۡمِ ٱلۡمَوۡعُودِ٢
Waalyawmi almawAAoodi
85:2And by the Promised Day
وَشَاهِدٖ وَمَشۡهُودٖ٣
Washahidin wamashhoodin
85:3And by the witness and that whereunto he beareth testimony
قُتِلَ أَصۡحَٰبُ ٱلۡأُخۡدُودِ٤
Qutila ashabu alukhdoodi
85:4(Self-)destroyed were the owners of the ditch
ٱلنَّارِ ذَاتِ ٱلۡوَقُودِ٥
Alnnari thati alwaqoodi
85:5Of the fuel-fed fire
إِذۡ هُمۡ عَلَيۡهَا قُعُودٞ٦
Ith hum AAalayha quAAoodun
85:6When they sat by it
وَهُمۡ عَلَىٰ مَا يَفۡعَلُونَ بِٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ شُهُودٞ٧
Wahum AAala ma yafAAaloona bialmumineena shuhoodun
85:7And were themselves the witnesses of what they did to the believers
وَمَا نَقَمُواْ مِنۡهُمۡ إِلَّآ أَن يُؤۡمِنُواْ بِٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَمِيدِ٨
Wama naqamoo minhum illa an yuminoo biAllahi alAAazeezi alhameedi
85:8They had naught against them save that they believed in Allah, the Mighty, the Owner of Praise
ٱلَّذِي لَهُۥ مُلۡكُ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِۚ وَٱللَّهُ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ شَهِيدٌ٩
Allathee lahu mulku alssamawati waalardi waAllahu AAala kulli shayin shaheedun
85:9Him unto Whom belongeth the Sovereignty of the heavens and the earth; and Allah is of all things the Witness
إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ فَتَنُواْ ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ وَٱلۡمُؤۡمِنَٰتِ ثُمَّ لَمۡ يَتُوبُواْ فَلَهُمۡ عَذَابُ جَهَنَّمَ وَلَهُمۡ عَذَابُ ٱلۡحَرِيقِ١٠
Inna allatheena fatanoo almumineena waalmuminati thumma lam yatooboo falahum AAathabu jahannama walahum AAathabu alhareeqi
85:10Lo! they who persecute believing men and believing women and repent not, theirs verily will be the doom of hell, and theirs the doom of burning
إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ لَهُمۡ جَنَّـٰتٞ تَجۡرِي مِن تَحۡتِهَا ٱلۡأَنۡهَٰرُۚ ذَٰلِكَ ٱلۡفَوۡزُ ٱلۡكَبِيرُ١١
Inna allatheena amanoo waAAamiloo alssalihati lahum jannatun tajree min tahtiha alanharu thalika alfawzu alkabeeru
85:11Lo! those who believe and do good works, theirs will be Gardens underneath which rivers flow. That is the Great Success
إِنَّ بَطۡشَ رَبِّكَ لَشَدِيدٌ١٢
Inna batsha rabbika lashadeedun
85:12Lo! the punishment of thy Lord is stern
إِنَّهُۥ هُوَ يُبۡدِئُ وَيُعِيدُ١٣
Innahu huwa yubdio wayuAAeedu
85:13Lo! He it is Who produceth, then reproduceth
وَهُوَ ٱلۡغَفُورُ ٱلۡوَدُودُ١٤
Wahuwa alghafooru alwadoodu
85:14And He is the Forgiving, the Loving
ذُو ٱلۡعَرۡشِ ٱلۡمَجِيدُ١٥
Thoo alAAarshi almajeedi
85:15Lord of the Throne of Glory
فَعَّالٞ لِّمَا يُرِيدُ١٦
FaAAAAalun lima yureedu
85:16Doer of what He will
هَلۡ أَتَىٰكَ حَدِيثُ ٱلۡجُنُودِ١٧
Hal ataka hadeethu aljunoodi
85:17Hath there come unto thee the story of the hosts
فِرۡعَوۡنَ وَثَمُودَ١٨
FirAAawna wathamooda
85:18Of Pharaoh and (the tribe of) Thamud
بَلِ ٱلَّذِينَ كَفَرُواْ فِي تَكۡذِيبٖ١٩
Bali allatheena kafaroo fee taktheebin
85:19Nay, but those who disbelieve live in denial
وَٱللَّهُ مِن وَرَآئِهِم مُّحِيطُۢ٢٠
WaAllahu min waraihim muheetun
85:20And Allah, all unseen, surroundeth them
بَلۡ هُوَ قُرۡءَانٞ مَّجِيدٞ٢١
Bal huwa quranun majeedun
85:21Nay, but it is a glorious Qur'an
فِي لَوۡحٖ مَّحۡفُوظِۭ٢٢
Fee lawhin mahfoothin
85:22On a guarded tablet
