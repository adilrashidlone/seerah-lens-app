سورة الفلق113. Al-Falaq · The Daybreak
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
قُلۡ أَعُوذُ بِرَبِّ ٱلۡفَلَقِ١
Qul aAAoothu birabbi alfalaqi
113:1Say: I seek refuge in the Lord of the Daybreak
مِن شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ٢
Min sharri ma khalaqa
113:2From the evil of that which He created
وَمِن شَرِّ غَاسِقٍ إِذَا وَقَبَ٣
Wamin sharri ghasiqin itha waqaba
113:3From the evil of the darkness when it is intense
وَمِن شَرِّ ٱلنَّفَّـٰثَٰتِ فِي ٱلۡعُقَدِ٤
Wamin sharri alnnaffathati fee alAAuqadi
113:4And from the evil of malignant witchcraft
وَمِن شَرِّ حَاسِدٍ إِذَا حَسَدَ٥
Wamin sharri hasidin itha hasada
113:5And from the evil of the envier when he envieth
