سورة عبس80. 'Abasa · He Frowned
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
عَبَسَ وَتَوَلَّىٰٓ١
AAabasa watawalla
80:1He frowned and turned away
أَن جَآءَهُ ٱلۡأَعۡمَىٰ٢
An jaahu alaAAma
80:2Because the blind man came unto him
وَمَا يُدۡرِيكَ لَعَلَّهُۥ يَزَّكَّىٰٓ٣
Wama yudreeka laAAallahu yazzakka
80:3What could inform thee but that he might grow (in grace)
أَوۡ يَذَّكَّرُ فَتَنفَعَهُ ٱلذِّكۡرَىٰٓ٤
Aw yaththakkaru fatanfaAAahu alththikra
80:4Or take heed and so the reminder might avail him
أَمَّا مَنِ ٱسۡتَغۡنَىٰ٥
Amma mani istaghna
80:5As for him who thinketh himself independent
فَأَنتَ لَهُۥ تَصَدَّىٰ٦
Faanta lahu tasadda
80:6Unto him thou payest regard
وَمَا عَلَيۡكَ أَلَّا يَزَّكَّىٰ٧
Wama AAalayka alla yazzakka
80:7Yet it is not thy concern if he grow not (in grace)
وَأَمَّا مَن جَآءَكَ يَسۡعَىٰ٨
Waamma man jaaka yasAAa
80:8But as for him who cometh unto thee with earnest purpose
وَهُوَ يَخۡشَىٰ٩
Wahuwa yakhsha
80:9And hath fear
فَأَنتَ عَنۡهُ تَلَهَّىٰ١٠
Faanta AAanhu talahha
80:10From him thou art distracted
كَلَّآ إِنَّهَا تَذۡكِرَةٞ١١
Kalla innaha tathkiratun
80:11Nay, but verily it is an Admonishment
فَمَن شَآءَ ذَكَرَهُۥ١٢
Faman shaa thakarahu
80:12So let whosoever will pay heed to it
فِي صُحُفٖ مُّكَرَّمَةٖ١٣
Fee suhufin mukarramatin
80:13On honoured leaves
مَّرۡفُوعَةٖ مُّطَهَّرَةِۭ١٤
MarfooAAatin mutahharatin
80:14Exalted, purified
بِأَيۡدِي سَفَرَةٖ١٥
Biaydee safaratin
80:15(Set down) by scribes
كِرَامِۭ بَرَرَةٖ١٦
Kiramin bararatin
80:16Noble and righteous
قُتِلَ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ مَآ أَكۡفَرَهُۥ١٧
Qutila alinsanu ma akfarahu
80:17Man is (self-)destroyed: how ungrateful
مِنۡ أَيِّ شَيۡءٍ خَلَقَهُۥ١٨
Min ayyi shayin khalaqahu
80:18From what thing doth He create him
مِن نُّطۡفَةٍ خَلَقَهُۥ فَقَدَّرَهُۥ١٩
Min nutfatin khalaqahu faqaddarahu
80:19From a drop of seed. He createth him and proportioneth him
ثُمَّ ٱلسَّبِيلَ يَسَّرَهُۥ٢٠
Thumma alssabeela yassarahu
80:20Then maketh the way easy for him
ثُمَّ أَمَاتَهُۥ فَأَقۡبَرَهُۥ٢١
Thumma amatahu faaqbarahu
80:21Then causeth him to die, and burieth him
ثُمَّ إِذَا شَآءَ أَنشَرَهُۥ٢٢
Thumma itha shaa ansharahu
80:22Then, when He will, He bringeth him again to life
كَلَّا لَمَّا يَقۡضِ مَآ أَمَرَهُۥ٢٣
Kalla lamma yaqdi ma amarahu
80:23Nay, but (man) hath not done what He commanded him
فَلۡيَنظُرِ ٱلۡإِنسَٰنُ إِلَىٰ طَعَامِهِۦٓ٢٤
Falyanthuri alinsanu ila taAAamihi
80:24Let man consider his food
أَنَّا صَبَبۡنَا ٱلۡمَآءَ صَبّٗا٢٥
Anna sababna almaa sabban
80:25How We pour water in showers
ثُمَّ شَقَقۡنَا ٱلۡأَرۡضَ شَقّٗا٢٦
Thumma shaqaqna alarda shaqqan
80:26Then split the earth in clefts
فَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا حَبّٗا٢٧
Faanbatna feeha habban
80:27And cause the grain to grow therein
وَعِنَبٗا وَقَضۡبٗا٢٨
WaAAinaban waqadban
80:28And grapes and green fodder
وَزَيۡتُونٗا وَنَخۡلٗا٢٩
Wazaytoonan wanakhlan
80:29And olive-trees and palm-trees
وَحَدَآئِقَ غُلۡبٗا٣٠
Wahadaiqa ghulban
80:30And garden-closes of thick foliage
وَفَٰكِهَةٗ وَأَبّٗا٣١
Wafakihatan waabban
80:31And fruits and grasses
مَّتَٰعٗا لَّكُمۡ وَلِأَنۡعَٰمِكُمۡ٣٢
MataAAan lakum walianAAamikum
80:32Provision for you and your cattle
فَإِذَا جَآءَتِ ٱلصَّآخَّةُ٣٣
Faitha jaati alssakhkhatu
80:33But when the Shout cometh
يَوۡمَ يَفِرُّ ٱلۡمَرۡءُ مِنۡ أَخِيهِ٣٤
Yawma yafirru almaro min akheehi
80:34On the day when a man fleeth from his brother
وَأُمِّهِۦ وَأَبِيهِ٣٥
Waommihi waabeehi
80:35And his mother and his father
وَصَٰحِبَتِهِۦ وَبَنِيهِ٣٦
Wasahibatihi wabaneehi
80:36And his wife and his children
لِكُلِّ ٱمۡرِيٕٖ مِّنۡهُمۡ يَوۡمَئِذٖ شَأۡنٞ يُغۡنِيهِ٣٧
Likulli imriin minhum yawmaithin shanun yughneehi
80:37Every man that day will have concern enough to make him heedless (of others)
وُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٖ مُّسۡفِرَةٞ٣٨
Wujoohun yawmaithin musfiratun
80:38On that day faces will be bright as dawn
ضَاحِكَةٞ مُّسۡتَبۡشِرَةٞ٣٩
Dahikatun mustabshiratun
80:39Laughing, rejoicing at good news
وَوُجُوهٞ يَوۡمَئِذٍ عَلَيۡهَا غَبَرَةٞ٤٠
Wawujoohun yawmaithin AAalayha ghabaratun
80:40And other faces, on that day, with dust upon them
تَرۡهَقُهَا قَتَرَةٌ٤١
Tarhaquha qataratun
80:41Veiled in darkness
أُوْلَـٰٓئِكَ هُمُ ٱلۡكَفَرَةُ ٱلۡفَجَرَةُ٤٢
Olaika humu alkafaratu alfajaratu
80:42Those are the disbelievers, the wicked
