سورة الإخلاص112. Al-Ikhlas · The Sincerity
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
قُلۡ هُوَ ٱللَّهُ أَحَدٌ١
Qul huwa Allahu ahadun
112:1Say: He is Allah, the One
ٱللَّهُ ٱلصَّمَدُ٢
Allahu alssamadu
112:2Allah, the eternally Besought of all
لَمۡ يَلِدۡ وَلَمۡ يُولَدۡ٣
Lam yalid walam yooladu
112:3He begetteth not nor was begotten
وَلَمۡ يَكُن لَّهُۥ كُفُوًا أَحَدُۢ٤
Walam yakun lahu kufuwan ahadun
112:4And there is none comparable unto Him
