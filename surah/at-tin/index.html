سورة التين95. At-Tin · The Fig
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلتِّينِ وَٱلزَّيۡتُونِ١
Waaltteeni waalzzaytooni
95:1By the fig and the olive
وَطُورِ سِينِينَ٢
Watoori seeneena
95:2By Mount Sinai
وَهَٰذَا ٱلۡبَلَدِ ٱلۡأَمِينِ٣
Wahatha albaladi alameeni
95:3And by this land made safe
لَقَدۡ خَلَقۡنَا ٱلۡإِنسَٰنَ فِيٓ أَحۡسَنِ تَقۡوِيمٖ٤
Laqad khalaqna alinsana fee ahsani taqweemin
95:4Surely We created man of the best stature
ثُمَّ رَدَدۡنَٰهُ أَسۡفَلَ سَٰفِلِينَ٥
Thumma radadnahu asfala safileena
95:5Then we reduced him to the lowest of the low
إِلَّا ٱلَّذِينَ ءَامَنُواْ وَعَمِلُواْ ٱلصَّـٰلِحَٰتِ فَلَهُمۡ أَجۡرٌ غَيۡرُ مَمۡنُونٖ٦
Illa allatheena amanoo waAAamiloo alssalihati falahum ajrun ghayru mamnoonin
95:6Save those who believe and do good works, and theirs is a reward unfailing
فَمَا يُكَذِّبُكَ بَعۡدُ بِٱلدِّينِ٧
Fama yukaththibuka baAAdu bialddeeni
95:7So who henceforth will give the lie to thee about the judgment
أَلَيۡسَ ٱللَّهُ بِأَحۡكَمِ ٱلۡحَٰكِمِينَ٨
Alaysa Allahu biahkami alhakimeena
95:8Is not Allah the most conclusive of all judges
