سورة التكوير81. At-Takwir · The Overthrowing
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
إِذَا ٱلشَّمۡسُ كُوِّرَتۡ١
Itha alshshamsu kuwwirat
81:1When the sun is overthrown
وَإِذَا ٱلنُّجُومُ ٱنكَدَرَتۡ٢
Waitha alnnujoomu inkadarat
81:2And when the stars fall
وَإِذَا ٱلۡجِبَالُ سُيِّرَتۡ٣
Waitha aljibalu suyyirat
81:3And when the hills are moved
وَإِذَا ٱلۡعِشَارُ عُطِّلَتۡ٤
Waitha alAAisharu AAuttilat
81:4And when the camels big with young are abandoned
وَإِذَا ٱلۡوُحُوشُ حُشِرَتۡ٥
Waitha alwuhooshu hushirat
81:5And when the wild beasts are herded together
وَإِذَا ٱلۡبِحَارُ سُجِّرَتۡ٦
Waitha albiharu sujjirat
81:6And when the seas rise
وَإِذَا ٱلنُّفُوسُ زُوِّجَتۡ٧
Waitha alnnufoosu zuwwijat
81:7And when souls are reunited
وَإِذَا ٱلۡمَوۡءُۥدَةُ سُئِلَتۡ٨
Waitha almawoodatu suilat
81:8And when the girl-child that was buried alive is asked
بِأَيِّ ذَنۢبٖ قُتِلَتۡ٩
Biayyi thanbin qutilat
81:9For what sin she was slain
وَإِذَا ٱلصُّحُفُ نُشِرَتۡ١٠
Waitha alssuhufu nushirat
81:10And when the pages are laid open
وَإِذَا ٱلسَّمَآءُ كُشِطَتۡ١١
Waitha alssamao kushitat
81:11And when the sky is torn away
وَإِذَا ٱلۡجَحِيمُ سُعِّرَتۡ١٢
Waitha aljaheemu suAAAAirat
81:12And when hell is lighted
وَإِذَا ٱلۡجَنَّةُ أُزۡلِفَتۡ١٣
Waitha aljannatu ozlifat
81:13And when the Garden is brought nigh
عَلِمَتۡ نَفۡسٞ مَّآ أَحۡضَرَتۡ١٤
AAalimat nafsun ma ahdarat
81:14(Then) every soul will know what it hath made ready
فَلَآ أُقۡسِمُ بِٱلۡخُنَّسِ١٥
Fala oqsimu bialkhunnasi
81:15Oh, but I call to witness the planets
ٱلۡجَوَارِ ٱلۡكُنَّسِ١٦
Aljawari alkunnasi
81:16The stars which rise and set
وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا عَسۡعَسَ١٧
Waallayli itha AAasAAasa
81:17And the close of night
وَٱلصُّبۡحِ إِذَا تَنَفَّسَ١٨
Waalssubhi itha tanaffasa
81:18And the breath of morning
إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولٖ كَرِيمٖ١٩
Innahu laqawlu rasoolin kareemin
81:19That this is in truth the word of an honoured messenger
ذِي قُوَّةٍ عِندَ ذِي ٱلۡعَرۡشِ مَكِينٖ٢٠
Thee quwwatin AAinda thee alAAarshi makeenin
81:20Mighty, established in the presence of the Lord of the Throne
مُّطَاعٖ ثَمَّ أَمِينٖ٢١
MutaAAin thamma ameenin
81:21(One) to be obeyed, and trustworthy
وَمَا صَاحِبُكُم بِمَجۡنُونٖ٢٢
Wama sahibukum bimajnoonin
81:22And your comrade is not mad
وَلَقَدۡ رَءَاهُ بِٱلۡأُفُقِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ٢٣
Walaqad raahu bialofuqi almubeeni
81:23Surely he beheld Him on the clear horizon
وَمَا هُوَ عَلَى ٱلۡغَيۡبِ بِضَنِينٖ٢٤
Wama huwa AAala alghaybi bidaneenin
81:24And he is not avid of the Unseen
وَمَا هُوَ بِقَوۡلِ شَيۡطَٰنٖ رَّجِيمٖ٢٥
Wama huwa biqawli shaytanin rajeemin
81:25Nor is this the utterance of a devil worthy to be stoned
فَأَيۡنَ تَذۡهَبُونَ٢٦
Faayna tathhaboona
81:26Whither then go ye
إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ٢٧
In huwa illa thikrun lilAAalameena
81:27This is naught else than a reminder unto creation
لِمَن شَآءَ مِنكُمۡ أَن يَسۡتَقِيمَ٢٨
Liman shaa minkum an yastaqeema
81:28Unto whomsoever of you willeth to walk straight
وَمَا تَشَآءُونَ إِلَّآ أَن يَشَآءَ ٱللَّهُ رَبُّ ٱلۡعَٰلَمِينَ٢٩
Wama tashaoona illa an yashaa Allahu rabbu alAAalameena
81:29And ye will not, unless (it be) that Allah willeth, the Lord of Creation
