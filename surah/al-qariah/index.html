سورة القارعة101. Al-Qari'ah · The Calamity
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ١
AlqariAAatu
101:1The Calamity
مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ٢
Ma alqariAAatu
101:2What is the Calamity
وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا ٱلۡقَارِعَةُ٣
Wama adraka ma alqariAAatu
101:3Ah, what will convey unto thee what the Calamity is
يَوۡمَ يَكُونُ ٱلنَّاسُ كَٱلۡفَرَاشِ ٱلۡمَبۡثُوثِ٤
Yawma yakoonu alnnasu kaalfarashi almabthoothi
101:4A day wherein mankind will be as thickly-scattered moths
وَتَكُونُ ٱلۡجِبَالُ كَٱلۡعِهۡنِ ٱلۡمَنفُوشِ٥
Watakoonu aljibalu kaalAAihni almanfooshi
101:5And the mountains will become as carded wool
فَأَمَّا مَن ثَقُلَتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ٦
Faamma man thaqulat mawazeenuhu
101:6Then, as for him whose scales are heavy (with good works)
فَهُوَ فِي عِيشَةٖ رَّاضِيَةٖ٧
Fahuwa fee AAeeshatin radiyatin
101:7He will live a pleasant life
وَأَمَّا مَنۡ خَفَّتۡ مَوَٰزِينُهُۥ٨
Waamma man khaffat mawazeenuhu
101:8But as for him whose scales are light
فَأُمُّهُۥ هَاوِيَةٞ٩
Faommuhu hawiyatun
101:9A bereft and Hungry One will be his mother
وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا هِيَهۡ١٠
Wama adraka ma hiyah
101:10Ah, what will convey unto thee what she is
نَارٌ حَامِيَةُۢ١١
Narun hamiyatun
101:11Raging Fire
