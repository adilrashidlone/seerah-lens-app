سورة الضحى93. Ad-Duhaa · The Morning Hours
بِسْمِ ٱللَّهِ ٱلرَّحْمَـٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
وَٱلضُّحَىٰ١
Waaldduha
93:1By the morning hours
وَٱلَّيۡلِ إِذَا سَجَىٰ٢
Waallayli itha saja
93:2And by the night when it is stillest
مَا وَدَّعَكَ رَبُّكَ وَمَا قَلَىٰ٣
Ma waddaAAaka rabbuka wama qala
93:3Thy Lord hath not forsaken thee nor doth He hate thee
وَلَلۡأٓخِرَةُ خَيۡرٞ لَّكَ مِنَ ٱلۡأُولَىٰ٤
Walalakhiratu khayrun laka mina aloola
93:4And verily the latter portion will be better for thee than the former
وَلَسَوۡفَ يُعۡطِيكَ رَبُّكَ فَتَرۡضَىٰٓ٥
Walasawfa yuAAteeka rabbuka fatarda
93:5And verily thy Lord will give unto thee so that thou wilt be content
أَلَمۡ يَجِدۡكَ يَتِيمٗا فَـَٔاوَىٰ٦
Alam yajidka yateeman faawa
93:6Did He not find thee an orphan and protect (thee)
وَوَجَدَكَ ضَآلّٗا فَهَدَىٰ٧
Wawajadaka dallan fahada
93:7Did He not find thee wandering and direct (thee)
وَوَجَدَكَ عَآئِلٗا فَأَغۡنَىٰ٨
Wawajadaka AAailan faaghna
93:8Did He not find thee destitute and enrich (thee)
فَأَمَّا ٱلۡيَتِيمَ فَلَا تَقۡهَرۡ٩
Faamma alyateema fala taqhar
93:9Therefor the orphan oppress not
وَأَمَّا ٱلسَّآئِلَ فَلَا تَنۡهَرۡ١٠
Waamma alssaila fala tanhar
93:10Therefor the beggar drive not away
وَأَمَّا بِنِعۡمَةِ رَبِّكَ فَحَدِّثۡ١١
Waamma biniAAmati rabbika fahaddith
93:11Therefor of the bounty of thy Lord be thy discourse
